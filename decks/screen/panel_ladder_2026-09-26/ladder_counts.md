## The short version

- The eight panel decks, matched exactly, are 10 of 55 games (18.2%). Counting the 5 variants that share a panel deck's core (four Mega Lucario ex decks with a different partner, Hydreigon / Mega Sableye ex), the panel family is 15 of 55 (27.3%).
- Butterfree / Mega Sceptile ex (4.95% on Limitless) is still not confirmed once (one unmapped game, Butterfree and Caterpie without Mega Sceptile ex, is on the ask list). TR Weezing ex / Hoopa ex never showed up.
- 29 of 55 games (52.7%) were mapped to a deck the panel does not have: 13 against other top-30 decks, 16 against listed decks outside the top 30; 1 game (1.8%) was a homebrew; **10 games (18.2%) are unmapped**.
- Dustin is 4-11 against panel decks (family view), 5-8 against other top-30 decks, 9-7 against off-list established decks, 0-1 against the homebrew, 8-2 against the unmapped games.
- The most-met deck outside the original eight is Dragonair Mega Rayquaza ex: 3 games, 2-1 (now the panel's eleventh list). Mega Charizard Y ex / Entei ex is next (3 mapped games, 0-3).
- 55 games is still a small sample. One game is 1.8 percentage points, so every share below has a wide margin. Treat them as a first read, not a measurement.

## Notes on the mapping

11 of the 45 mapped games (not counting the 10 unmapped) are inferences from partial information (marked `likely` in the CSV). The Sept 26 ones:

- "Darkrai espeon sleep" -> Mega Altaria ex Espeon. Espeon B3a (Hypnoblast, sleep) plus Darkrai B2b (Bad Dreams, 20 damage to a sleeping Active each turn) is the sleep engine of the #2 deck, and both cards are in the panel Altaria list. The Altaria itself was never seen. Without this row the altaria strict count is 1.
- "magikarp/carvanha" -> Mega Sharpedo ex Gyarados. Those are the two basics of that top-30 deck; the opponent conceded before evolving.
- "Charizard ex / entei ex" -> Mega Charizard Y ex Entei ex. Same Fire deck; whether the Charizard was the Mega or the plain ex was not recorded.
- "igglybuff/darkrai/milotic" -> Milotic ex Igglybuff (a listed sleep deck), not the top-30 Milotic ex Eevee ex, because no Eevee ex was named.
- "eevee/froakie-greninja ex" -> Vaporeon ex Greninja ex (listed). The Eevee never evolved.
- "lucario ex and team rocket raticate ex" -> Team Rocket's Raticate ex Mega Lucario ex (listed). Kept off the Lucario family because the Raticate energy-steal plan is the deck's engine.
- "Hydreigon / bombirdier" -> Hydreigon Mega Absol ex (the skeptic's Sept 26 fix: an inference, not an exact match).

Added Sept 28 night (all `likely`; none touches a panel or ten-list weight):

- "Oricorio / Team Rocket’s Magmar / Wurmple" -> Dustox Team Rocket's Magmar. same cards as the earlier Dustox / TR Magmar game (Wurmple line, TR Magmar); Oricorio is an extra; listed (5 rows).
- "Oricorio / Dragonite" -> Dragonite Oricorio. only two Pokemon seen; listed as Dragonite Oricorio (5 rows), outside top 30.
- "Oricorio / Magneton / Miraidon ex" -> Magnezone Miraidon ex. top-30 #13; Magneton evolves into Magnezone (not seen); Oricorio is an extra.
- "Dragonite / Wynaut / Mime Jr." -> Dragonite ex. nearest listed deck is Dragonite ex (41 rows), outside top 30; the partner was not confirmed.

Variant rule: a deck is a "panel variant" when its main attacker line is a panel deck's core and only the partner differs (Mega Lucario ex with Hitmontop, Dugtrio or Hitmonchan ex; Hydreigon with Mega Sableye ex). TR Weezing ex with TR Magmar was taken out on Sept 28 (ruling P3): the card that differs is the attacker. Exact-name counts are given everywhere so nobody has to accept that rule to use the numbers.

Mapping rule for a new game (Sept 28 night): it goes on a panel or ten-list deck only if the opponent text names that deck's defining Pokemon or the archetype itself. A text that shows only support cards (Riolu without Mega Lucario ex, Butterfree without Mega Sceptile ex, Entei ex without a Charizard) is `unmapped`, not guessed.

"Established, off-list" means a deck name that appears in the Sept 25 Limitless match pull but not in the Sept 10 top 30; the match-row count for each is in the CSV's reason column.
