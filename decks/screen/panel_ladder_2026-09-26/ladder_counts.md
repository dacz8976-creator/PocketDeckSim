# Ladder opponents vs the eight-deck panel (Sept 26, 2026)

Source: the Ladder Log artifact database (collection `logs`, read Sept 26), 10 deck documents,
33 games, Sept 15 to Sept 24. The Sept 24 CSV copy has the same 33 games row for row. Dates are
Dustin-local (as in that CSV); the artifact stores UTC timestamps, kept in the `ts` column of
`ladder_mapping.csv`. Archetype names are the Limitless `deck_name` spellings from the Sept 25
match pull; top-30 ranks and shares are from `limitless_2026-09-10.json`.

Overall record: 12-21 (36%).

## The short version

- The eight panel decks, matched exactly, are 10 of 33 games (30%). Counting the five variants
  that share a panel deck's core (three Mega Lucario ex decks with a different partner,
  Hydreigon / Mega Sableye ex, TR Weezing ex / TR Magmar), the panel covers 15 of 33 (45%).
- Butterfree / Mega Sceptile ex (4.95% on Limitless) never showed up. TR Weezing ex / Hoopa ex
  never showed up in its Limitless form, only the Magmar variant, once.
- 17 of 33 games (52%) were established decks the panel does not have: 8 against other top-30
  decks, 9 against listed decks outside the top 30. Only 1 game (3%) was a homebrew.
- Dustin is 5-10 against panel decks (family view), 3-5 against other top-30 decks, 4-5 against
  off-list established decks, 0-1 against the homebrew.
- 33 games is a small sample. One game is 3 percentage points, so every share below has a wide
  margin. Treat them as a first read, not a measurement.

## Proposed panel weights

As asked: each archetype faced at least twice, as a share of all 33 logged games, plus the
eight panel decks' shares.

Archetypes faced at least twice (exact names): Mega Altaria ex Espeon 6.1%, Hydreigon Mega
Absol ex 6.1%, Vespiquen ex Shuckle ex 6.1%, Mega Blaziken ex 6.1%, Mega Charizard Y ex Entei ex
6.1%, Mega Sharpedo ex Gyarados 6.1%. Grouped by family: Mega Lucario ex 12.1%, Hydreigon 9.1%,
Dustox 6.1%, the rest as above.

Eight panel decks, share of all games (family view): lucario 12.1%, altaria 6.1%, sceptile 0%,
vespiquen 6.1%, suicune 3.0%, weezing 3.0%, blaziken 6.1%, hydreigon 9.1%.

My suggestion, not a decision: with 33 games the pure ladder weights swing too hard on single
games (Sceptile at 0% after 33 games is not evidence it is gone, and Hydreigon at 20% is three
games). The 50/50 blend keeps the ladder's message (more Lucario and Hydreigon, less Suicune and
Weezing than Limitless says) without zeroing anything. Also worth knowing: the two largest
off-panel repeats are Mega Charizard Y ex / Entei ex (2 games, 0-2) and Mega Sharpedo ex /
Gyarados (2 games, 2-0).

## Notes on the mapping

Six of the 33 mappings are inferences from partial information (marked `likely` in the CSV):

- "Darkrai espeon sleep" -> Mega Altaria ex Espeon. Espeon B3a (Hypnoblast, sleep) plus Darkrai
  B2b (Bad Dreams, 20 damage to a sleeping Active each turn) is the sleep engine of the #2 deck,
  and both cards are in the panel Altaria list. The Altaria itself was never seen. Without this
  row the altaria strict count is 1 (3.0%).
- "magikarp/carvanha" -> Mega Sharpedo ex Gyarados. Those are the two basics of that top-30 deck;
  the opponent conceded before evolving.
- "Charizard ex / entei ex" -> Mega Charizard Y ex Entei ex. Same Fire deck; whether the
  Charizard was the Mega or the plain ex was not recorded.
- "igglybuff/darkrai/milotic" -> Milotic ex Igglybuff (a listed sleep deck), not the top-30
  Milotic ex Eevee ex, because no Eevee ex was named.
- "eevee/froakie-greninja ex" -> Vaporeon ex Greninja ex (listed). The Eevee never evolved.
- "lucario ex and team rocket raticate ex" -> Team Rocket's Raticate ex Mega Lucario ex (listed).
  Kept off the Lucario family because the Raticate energy-steal plan is the deck's engine.

Variant rule: a deck is a "panel variant" when its main attacker line is a panel deck's core and
only the partner differs (Mega Lucario ex with Hitmontop, Dugtrio or Hitmonchan ex; Hydreigon
with Mega Sableye ex; TR Weezing ex with TR Magmar). Exact-name counts are given everywhere so
nobody has to accept that rule to use the numbers.

"Established, off-list" means a deck name that appears in the Sept 25 Limitless match pull but
not in the Sept 10 top 30; the match-row count for each is in the CSV's reason column.
