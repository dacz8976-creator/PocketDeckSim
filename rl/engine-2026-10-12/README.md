- **What it is:** rules switch 2, on main-8626a35's engine (`../engine-2026-10-02/`). The engine now follows the plain card text in three more places, plus one text fix (`../results/engine_switch_rules2_2026-10/README.md`; `rules/09`, "Fixed Oct 12"):
  - **Round 2's coin-prevention rules and the card-text fixes.** Wild Swing and six other sites (Wellspring Dance, Tornado Shot, Double Splash and Triple Bombardment, Mischievous Ring, Litter, and Mega Kangaskhan ex's second punch) now flip Meowth's, Togekiss's, Bastiodon's and Hisuian Goodra's coin when their damage lands, and so does any other attack's queued plain hit. The coin Abilities and Guts also answer an attack's damage to its own side's Pokémon, and Perish Body flips for a queued hit. Will works for a Confused attacker and on a block coin ("If tails, that attack doesn't happen"), and Victory Star is offered after that coin's heads and with Will pending. Each Ariados adds 1 to the Retreat Cost. Luxury Coin works only on the player's own Stadium, and a Fossil can't be played under an Item lock.
  - **Fossils are Item cards** at the seven other places that read "Item card" (P3).
  - **Return damage left by an attack takes Weakness:** Cursed Jewel, Spike Armor, Bristling Spikes, Needle Lariat and Shell Trap (+20; doubled under Bounded Field unless the holder is a Mega Evolution Pokémon ex; `rules/02` §2). A Tool's or an Ability's return damage (Rocky Helmet, Rough Skin) stays flat.
  - **P1:** Gholdengo's caveat text (no game changes). Victini's caveat is rewritten to match the new rules.
  - 26 engine files change, 17 source and 9 test files (`../results/engine_switch_rules2_2026-10/allowed_engine_files.tsv`). The suite at P: 2,103 passed, 0 failed.

None is set by default, so every rule above is on. Set one and the program is another engine: never set one in a recorded run. They exist for step 8c's revert check (turning a rule off must bring the old move back).
