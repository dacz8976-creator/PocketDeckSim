- **What it is:** main-c045ede's engine (`../engine-2026-10-12/`) plus these changes (`../results/engine_candidate_2026-10-20/PLAN.md`, written before the builds, with its Oct 20 addendum; `rules/09`, "Fixed Oct 21"):
  - **The Growl-to-0 rule** (501fc1dd): an attack that an attacker-side effect (Growl, Teary Attack and the like) cuts to 0 does 0: no Weakness, and the Bench hit is cut too, as recorded on Oct 19 (`rules/02` §1, §3; `rules/05` #11). A defender-side "+X damage" effect is skipped as well when the cut reaches 0, and lands when the cut leaves damage, as recorded on Oct 20 (the Luxray/Espathra retry; `rules/05` #11); tests pin both.
  - **The hit back as attack damage** (a1d3a714; Dustin, Oct 20: "The attack back is treated as an attack in every situation (weakness, attack blocking, bounded field) not like an ability. Abilities don't trigger heavy helmet, attacks do"; `rules/02` §4, `rules/05` #31). The hit back left by Cursed Jewel, Spike Armor, Bristling Spikes, Needle Lariat and Shell Trap keeps its Weakness and Bounded Field and now meets the Attacking Pokémon's own cards: Safeguard and the Bench shields prevent it; Intimidating Fang cuts it before Weakness; Heavy Helmet, Steel Apron, Metal Core Barrier and the damage-cut Abilities cut it after Weakness (the recorded Heavy Helmet case: 30 to 10); Disguise prevents what is left; and when it still damages the Active, that Pokémon's Rocky Helmet (upstream #367, with #418's test), its 20-damage Ability and its Poison Barb or Poison Point answer the holder. Of these, only Weakness, Bounded Field and Heavy Helmet are recorded and only Rocky Helmet is named in the ruling; the rest apply the ruling to each card's text, kept on Oct 21 (`../results/engine_candidate_2026-10-20/RELEASE_NOTES.md`, "Readings of his Oct 20 ruling applied to card text").
  - **Upstream #385** (9c51453b): "Once during your turn, when you put this Pokémon from your hand onto your Bench" Abilities (Miraidon ex, Roaring Moon, Poltchageist) are offered only on the owner's turn.
  - **Upstream #390** (75343578): random multi-hit damage is enumerated in a fixed order, so a replay is deterministic.
  - **Upstream #401, the Fossil part** (42d2dd8e): DiscardFossil is offered once, not twice.
  - **Upstream #344, narrow** (764f7459): a deck whose Energy zone holds Dragon or Colorless Energy is invalid, with a named panic.
  - Tests: the ported upstream tests (ccb1d3b5), the "+X" pin and Poltchageist's turn tests (6726f83f), and the hit back's own (a1d3a714). `engine/UPSTREAM.md` was refreshed (d9d2e704); its line 9 still says "NOT adopted" and lists #367/#418 as deferred, both out of date: it stays as built until a later commit.

- **Revert switches:** none new. The engine reads the same 14 `DECKGYM_*` environment variables as main-c045ede, none set by default. Set one and the program is another engine: never set one in a recorded run. None of the changes above has a switch of its own, but the hit back's change rides on rules switch 2's return-damage switch: `DECKGYM_FLAT_RETURN_DAMAGE` or `DECKGYM_ROUND2_OFF` turns all of it off (a flat hit back with no Weakness, cut, prevention or answer), as either turned off the hit back's Weakness before.
