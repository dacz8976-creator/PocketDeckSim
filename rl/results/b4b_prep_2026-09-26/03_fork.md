## Bottom line

1. **A set enters the engine as data, then as code.** `engine/database.json` is the source; `engine/src/bin/card_enum_generator.rs` regenerates `engine/src/card_ids.rs` (the `CardId` enum plus the `"B4a 001"` -> `CardId::B4a001Volbeat` map) and `engine/src/database.rs` (the card structs). Recipe: `engine/README.md` lines 204-234. Upstream does it as one commit touching exactly those three files: `edf22f2` "B4" (2026-07-29, in this repo's history: `database.json` +5,435, `src/card_ids.rs` +482, `src/database.rs` +5,382/-6). The glob-path history of `card_ids.rs` is one such commit per set ("B2 Cards", "B2a", "Add Promo-B", "B2b Cards", "B3 Cards", "B3a Cards", "Everyday Wonders", "B4"; git5.sh).
2. **Pokémon attacks and abilities, Tools and Stadiums are matched by printed text, so a reprint is playable as soon as its data is in.** `EFFECT_MECHANIC_MAP` and `EFFECT_ABILITY_MECHANIC_MAP` are keyed by the effect string (`engine/src/card_validation.rs` lines 50 and 58); `has_tool`/`tool_count`/`tool_effects_equal` compare effect text (`engine/src/tools.rs` lines 95-110, comment at 104: "including different printings"); `has_stadium` compares effect text (`engine/src/stadiums.rs` line 106).
3. **Supporters and Items are matched by `CardId`, so every B4b reprint of one needs a new match arm** in `engine/src/actions/apply_trainer_action.rs` (fallback `_ => panic!("Unsupported Trainer Card")`, line 329) and `engine/src/move_generation/move_generation_trainer.rs` (fallback `_ => None`, line 345). The pattern already exists for the A4b reprint set: `… | CardId::A4b379RareCandy => Outcomes::single_fn(rare_candy_effect)` (apply_trainer_action.rs line 155) and `… => can_play_rare_candy(state, trainer_card)` (move_generation_trainer.rs line 133). For B4b that is at least Copycat and Elesa (`01_set.md` lines 112-113: new-art ☆☆ reprints of B1 225 and B3b 066).
4. **A handful of other places enumerate printings by id and would silently miss a reprint**: `engine/src/card_validation.rs` lines 89-113 (the RulesUnverified list; Victini B3 025 is on B4b's parallel-foil list), `engine/src/actions/apply_action_helpers.rs` line 638 (Haxorus, also on the foil list, `01_set.md` line 133), `engine/src/actions/trainer_coin_plan.rs` lines 46-101 (coin-flip Trainers and the Mesagoza/Arcade activators), `engine/src/players/public_reply.rs` lines 617-627 and 673-681 (Dragalge ex is a ◊◊◊◊ card, so expected in B4b, `01_set.md` line 147), `engine/src/state/mod.rs` line 365 (Misty). Section 3 lists them with a grep that finds them.
5. **B4a got in by the fork's own import, not by a merge.** The fork's B4a data files are upstream's (`02_upstream.md` section 5), the implementations were built in the Sept 5-6 "cards2 … cards8" candidate series (`project_manifest.json` lines 24-94, `engine/UPSTREAM.md` lines 16-23), and the whole thing was squashed into `12938ca` (2026-09-09) on the old fork repo, then subtree-added to this repo as `02a5b24` (2026-09-24). Details in section 4.

## 5. The two databases, the canon map and the catalog
