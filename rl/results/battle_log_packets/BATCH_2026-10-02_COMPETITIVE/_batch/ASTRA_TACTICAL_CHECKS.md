## 1. 020920: delaying Wailmer evolution

**Accepted as a legal local opportunity, not a demonstrated alternate outcome.** Keeping the original Wailmer unevolved through owner turn 6 would allow the third Water to pay Wave Splash's three-Water cost. Against the same visible Active Charmeleon, its 60 damage plus the printed Water weakness of 20 equals 80, enough to knock out Charmeleon at its displayed 80 HP.

The actual turn-4 evolution leaves a two-Water Wailord with a four-Colorless attack cost. The actual turn-6 third attachment therefore still cannot power Whale Pump. The inspected turn-6 frame also shows the second Wailmer's printed three-Water, 60-damage Wave Splash, corroborating the attack in this recording. Charmeleon has no visible defensive tool or damage-reduction effect in the inspected state.

The visible Watch Over effect can heal 20 from whichever owner Pokémon is Active without needing Energy on Indeedee. Thus leaving the first Wailmer at 100 maximum HP would not forfeit that healing option. It does forfeit Wailord's immediate larger HP buffer. The report says the opponent makes no turn-5 attack; that actual sequence cannot be guaranteed after changing the owner's evolution choice. Even the earlier visible Slash is only 40 before Helmet, but hidden options and different opponent decisions remain unknown.

**Timing qualification:** To use Wave Splash on turn 6, the owner must keep Wailmer unevolved until that attack. Evolution could happen on a later owner turn, ordinarily turn 8, if Wailmer survives. Do not describe an attack followed by evolution in the same turn.

## 2. 021402: Indeedee as the Sabrina replacement

**Accepted as a legal local opportunity, conditional on the same observed opposing continuation.** The full-HP Indeedee ex was an available replacement. Against the observed Fantasia Force for 110, its 130 HP would leave 20 HP. Its weakness is Darkness, so this Psychic attack does not add weakness damage. Indeedee's printed retreat cost is one, while the active Peculiar Plaza reduces Psychic Pokémon's retreat costs by two. It can therefore retreat without an Energy attachment while Plaza remains in play.

A concrete next-turn sequence is: optionally heal Indeedee while it is Active, retreat it under Plaza, promote the three-Water non-ex Wailord, attach the fourth Water to Wailord, then use Whale Pump for 100. Against the same full-HP Mega Gardevoir ex, that would leave 110 HP, not score a knockout. Attaching Water before the retreat would also be legal, provided it goes to Wailord and the retreat occurs before Plaza is replaced.

**Sequencing matters:** The actual owner turn replaces Plaza with Soothing Shore. In this alternative, retreat Indeedee first. Otherwise the free retreat disappears, and an unenergized Indeedee cannot pay its restored one-Energy retreat cost without spending the turn's attachment or another resource. The actual zero-Energy Wailord ex does not receive Plaza's Psychic-type discount and has a printed four-Energy retreat cost. Choosing it creates the visible mobility problem.

**Healing and attack limits:** Watch Over can heal the Active Indeedee itself for 20 on the owner's turn, giving 40 HP before retreat, and requires no attached Energy. Lucky Ice Pop's first 20-point heal does not require winning a coin flip; a heads result is needed to reuse that copy. The favorable sequence recorded on Wailord ex is not guaranteed in the counterfactual. Watch Over and the visible Lucky Ice Pop target the Active Pokémon, so using them after Indeedee retreats would heal Wailord instead. Soothing Shore heals Pokémon with Water attached at the end of their owner's turn; it would not heal the unenergized benched Indeedee in this line. None of these heals is needed merely to survive the already observed 110 hit and retreat next turn. An unenergized Indeedee also cannot use its two-Psychic attack; this line uses it for survival and retreat, then attacks with Wailord.

The injured Indeedee remains a two-point target on the Bench, and the opponent is visibly building powerful Psychic attackers. The 03:31 inspection shows Mega Diancie's expected damage already at 240, illustrating why an earlier 100-damage attack is not proof of a won game. Hidden cards, alternate attack choices after Sabrina, future Energy allocation, targeting and healing remain unknown. Accept the available earlier attack, not a claim that this necessarily reverses the loss.

## 3. 023418: Bonsly reduction after Cyrus

**Accepted rules conclusion: the prospective attack is 80 damage, not 110. The damage itself was not executed in the footage.** Bonsly's Teary Attack says that during the opponent's next turn, attacks used by the Defending Pokémon do 30 less damage. That affected Pokémon is the owner's Wailord. Wailord remains Active when Cyrus switches the opposing Bonsly with the damaged Mega Lucario ex, so changing the target does not remove the effect on Wailord.

Five Water makes Whale Pump 60 + 50 = 110 before the reduction, then 110 - 30 = 80. Mega Lucario ex has Psychic weakness, not Water weakness, and is shown at 110 HP. The prospective hit therefore leaves 30 HP. The downward effect icon remains visible on Wailord at the concession. Healing Wailord's HP does not erase this attack effect.

The official Pocket Battle Rules FAQ explains that effects on a Pokémon are removed when that Pokémon goes to the Bench. This supports the distinction here: Cyrus moves the opponent's Pokémon; it does not bench the affected Wailord. The conclusion rests on the printed Bonsly/Cyrus text, unchanged Wailord position, and retained effect indicator, with the FAQ corroborating the relevant rule. [Pokémon Support: Battle Rules FAQ, Big Beat example](https://support.pokemon.com/hc/en-us/articles/38906248102292-Pok%C3%A9mon-TCG-Pocket-Battle-Rules-FAQ), retrieved October 2, 2026 UTC.

Do not describe this as a demonstrated lethal Cyrus play or an observed three-point Mega knockout. The observed result is an opponent concession before Wailord attacks. The opponent's reason for conceding remains unknown.

My independent read of the larger, tilted card in [021402 at 03:00.000](../20261002_021402000_iOS_competitive_sol/native/t0180.00.jpg) is **Wondrous Waves**, consistent with the prior Energy check. The smaller artwork-overlaid text is less suitable for name transcription. Do not silently replace the name with Voracious Waves on this evidence. The verified four-Water cost, 100 damage and recovery from Special Conditions are unaffected, and none of the three tactical conclusions depends on the attack's English name.
