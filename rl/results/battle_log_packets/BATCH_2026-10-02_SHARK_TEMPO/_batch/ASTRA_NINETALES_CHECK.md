# Binding Snow and Psy Shadow: targeted check

Checked October 2, 2026 by Astra. Source: `20261002_143309000_iOS.MP4`, SHA-256 `3358738fb441f5ca54931447b3c6b11fb6063255caa13f7406f1ec0e3e29c461`. This is a bounded mechanics and state check, not a second full review. It concerns 143309 only.

**Printed-rule conclusion:** Binding Snow covers Psy Shadow's proposed attachment to the Active Pokemon. The prohibition is not limited to the normal attachment. It says the opponent cannot take any Energy from the Energy Zone to attach to their Active Pokemon during their next turn. Psy Shadow takes Psychic Energy from that same zone and attaches it to the Psychic Pokemon in the Active Spot. Its source and destination therefore match the prohibited action exactly.

The printed effect applies to the opposing player for that next turn. Replacing their Active Pokemon does not change the prohibited source and destination. It does not prohibit normal attachments to their Bench. Nor does it remove Energy already attached. Psy Shadow itself only targets the Active Spot, so it cannot simply redirect its attachment to a Benched Mewtwo under this text.

## Appropriate description of this game

Ninetales repeatedly used Binding Snow while the opponent's Active Mewtwo remained unpowered and the Gardevoir line received Energy on the Bench. That visible resource pattern is consistent with Binding Snow denying both normal Energy-Zone attachment and Psy Shadow attachment to the Active Pokemon. The first-pass review reports no Psy Shadow activation, attempted activation or rejection, so do not say the footage directly demonstrates a failed ability attempt. The card-text interaction is established separately from the observed resource state.

A prospective way to plan around this restriction is to build an attacker with normal attachments while it is still Benched, then bring it Active after it has enough Energy. This is a general legal avenue, not proof that the opponent had a successful escape available in this particular hand. Mewtwo's visible attacks require at least two Energy; the observed zero-Energy Mewtwo could not attack or pay its printed two-Energy retreat cost. Available switching effects and the opponent's reasoning are unknown. Do not label the opponent's policy erroneous or infer intent from the absence of a Psy Shadow animation.
