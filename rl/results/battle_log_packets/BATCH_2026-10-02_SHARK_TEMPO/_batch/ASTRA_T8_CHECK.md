## Legal alternative and its limits

On T8, the owner could retain the observed evolution and attach the normal Water to the second Ninetales, then retreat the first Ninetales by discarding its two Water and promote Lapras. The attachment does not need to pay retreat, because the old Ninetales already has enough Energy. Retreat could also occur before that attachment. Either order must finish before attacking. Lapras then uses Surf for 90, leaving Chandelure at 50 HP.

If the opponent makes the same observed next-turn attachment, Slow Sear and Heat Blast, Lapras survives at 30 HP because Fire does not exploit its Lightning weakness. No knockout point is awarded on that turn. A second Surf then knocks out the remaining 50-HP Chandelure on T10, taking the owner to two points while the opponent remains at zero. The second Ninetales can still receive its T8 Water and be attack-ready, as in the actual game. No Lapras retreat is needed in this short continuation.

Costs and differences: the damaged ex remains a two-point target on the Bench, now without Energy; Lapras takes damage earlier; Surf deals 10 less than Binding Snow and does not renew the Energy-Zone-to-Active restriction. The opponent may respond differently to all of this. Unknown gust, healing, switching or other resources cannot be assumed absent or present. Even the same later Will/Primal Wingbeat line is a conditional continuation, not a forced response. Preserve the actual win and describe this as a supported point-preservation alternative rather than a proven superior result.
