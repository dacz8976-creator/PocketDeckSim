## Supported observations

### 20261001_203626000_iOS.MP4

- After Will guaranteed the initial first coin as heads, Victory Star rerolled it to tails. The guarantee was not applied again to the replacement sequence.
- Zangoose Counterattack returned 20 damage after a lethal hit, producing two knockouts and one point each. A zero-damage attack produced no return damage.
- With two Victini present, an attack received one Victory Star reroll. Printed text explicitly limits the ability to one use per turn.
- Bounded Field doubled a one-head Double Smash from 40 to 80 against Fire-weak ordinary Pokemon.

Limits: No Confused Pokemon attempts an attack in this recording. The Mega Evolution ex exception, non-Fire attacks and broader Will interactions were not tested. Hidden cards remain unknown; test choices are not competitive-policy grades.

### 20261001_204634000_iOS.MP4

- Victory Star replaced Heat Charged 0 heads/3 tails with 2 heads/1 tail. One starting Fire became three; the original coin batch did not add Energy.
- Umbreon Dark Binding blocked a Basic from attacking. A new Active could attack after retreat, and evolving the affected Darumaka to Darmanitan cleared its block while preserving 40 damage.
- Flame Patch supplied discarded Fire to the new Active Darumaka after the normal turn attachment had already been used.
- Greedent healed from 80 to 90 with Leftovers at the end of its turn while active.

Limits: Failed Confusion checks and Will with Victory Star were not tested here. Bounded Field was present, but this recording did not test its numerical weakness multiplier. Hidden cards and exact obstructed animation digits remain qualified.

### 20261001_205658000_iOS.MP4

- Victory Star replaced Heat Charged 1H/2T with another 1H/2T; the Active gained one Fire rather than adding both batches. Later-turn prompts and kept original results matched settled Energy.
- Netherwing required three Fire plus one Colorless. The observed attempt with only two Fire was refused.
- Double Smash kept two heads for 80, then replaced two tails with one head/one tail for 40. Settled HP changes matched both results.
- Flame Patch added discarded Fire to Active Darmanitan, while a distinct ordinary attachment went to Benched Moltres.
- Two ordinary ex knockouts awarded two points each; the opponent score display stopped at three.

Limits: Only one Victini was in play; multiple-Victini enforcement was not tested. Will and Team Rockets Master Plan were drawn or inspected and not played. Successful Netherwing, matching weakness multiplication, Disguise, and settled terminal Fantasia Force Energy allocation were not tested.
