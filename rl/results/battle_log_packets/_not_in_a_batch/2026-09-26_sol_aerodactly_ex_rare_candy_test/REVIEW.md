# Aerodactyl ex / Rare Candy: on-screen transcript

**Scope:** Multi-turn mechanic test ending during play, not a completed battle. The Auto switch is visible and off during sampled play. Opponent automation and Solo/PvP mode are not established. No spoken narration: the AAC track is digital silence (mean/max -91 dB); this transcript covers on-screen events.

## Observed timeline

- **00:00–00:45:** Owner's Druddigon (100 HP) faces opponent's Hitmonchan (80 HP); opponent benches Marshadow and Old Amber. Owner benches Dratini and plays Rainbow Cave. Rare Candy and Haxorus later become visible in the owner's hand.
- **01:12–01:16:** Opponent evolves the Benched Old Amber into Aerodactyl ex. Its visible **Primeval Law** text says the opponent cannot play Pokémon from hand to evolve the opponent's Active Pokémon [72s](evidence/72s.jpg). A “Can't play Pokémon from the hand to evolve Active Pokémon” banner appears immediately afterward [75s](evidence/75s.jpg). No owner card play causing that banner is visible at this instant.
- **01:20–01:45:** Hitmonchan attacks Druddigon and Druddigon's Rough Skin activates. Owner evolves Benched Dratini into Dragonair while Aerodactyl ex remains on the opponent's Bench [105s](evidence/105s.jpg).
- **02:15–02:48:** Owner uses Dragonair's Dragon's Blessing, later Knocks Out Hitmonchan for the first point. Opponent brings Aerodactyl ex to the Active Spot. Owner benches an Axew.
- **03:00–03:48:** A second Old Amber is benched. Aerodactyl ex attacks; Druddigon survives at 10 HP. Owner places a second Axew on the Bench [232s](evidence/232s.jpg) and switches the older Axew to the Active Spot. At 03:59 the board shows Active Axew (70 HP), Benched Druddigon (10 HP), second Axew (70 HP), Dragonair (80 HP), and opponent Active Aerodactyl ex (100 HP). Rare Candy and two Haxorus are in hand [239s](evidence/239s.jpg).
- **04:00–04:12:** Owner selects Rare Candy. The game displays “The conditions for using this Item card have not been met” [240s](evidence/240s.jpg). Owner inspects Aerodactyl ex, then Dragonair. At the end, Axew is still Active and Rare Candy plus both Haxorus remain in hand; Axew has gained a Fighting Energy since the preselection frame [252s](evidence/252s.jpg). No match result appears before the 04:17.8 cutoff.

**Inference:** Primeval Law is a plausible cause for Rare Candy being unusable on Active Axew. The newly placed Benched Axew is separately ineligible for Rare Candy that turn under the printed Item text, and Dragonair is already Stage 1. Therefore the generic Item error alone does **not** isolate which eligibility check the app evaluated first. The earlier 01:15 banner follows Aerodactyl's entrance and is not evidence of an attempted player evolution. Benched Dratini-to-Dragonair evolution does show a different outcome for a Bench target while Aerodactyl ex was in play.

**Unknown:** Exact target the owner intended when selecting Rare Candy, the app's internal reason for the generic Item rejection, and the unfinished battle result.
