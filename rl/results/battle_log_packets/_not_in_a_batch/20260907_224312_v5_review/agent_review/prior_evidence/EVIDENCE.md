# Mega Sableye ex / Cursed Jewel focused evidence

## Disposition

The focused sequence supersedes intake candidate 4's label. This is a **direct Cursed Jewel knockout followed by the attack's delayed-effect callout**, not a demonstrated retaliation knockout.

At `227.500`, the opponent has 2 points and a full-HP Mega Sableye ex Active at 170 HP. The owner's Active is Gabite at 60 remaining HP (80 printed maximum), with no Benched Pokémon. `228.500` identifies this as the opponent's turn 10. Mega Sableye ex then uses Cursed Jewel. The card close-up at `234.000` shows 80 printed damage and the next-turn conditional 40-damage text. `235.000` displays 80 damage on Gabite, `236.000` shows Gabite at 0 while Mega Sableye ex remains at 170, and `238.000` displays “If damaged by an attack, do 40 to the Attacking Pokémon.” That callout announces the effect created for the owner's next turn; no 40-damage event occurs here. The opponent receives point 3 at `241.500`–`242.000`, and `244.000` shows the owner's Defeat result.

## Board details relevant to damage

- Mega Sableye ex is the attacker and remains at 170 HP through the knockout/effect callout. Two attached Energy indicators are visible and satisfy the printed Darkness plus Colorless cost. No attached Tool indicator is visible.
- Gabite is at 60/80 HP before the attack, has the Water and Fighting Energy needed for Linear Attack attached, and has no visible Tool. The eval14 database identifies this print as `B4a 053` from its unique visible combination of 80 HP, Dragon type, and Linear Attack text.
- Rainbow Cave is the Stadium in play (`B4 155`). Its database effect changes the Energy Zone and has no damage modifier.
- Gabite has no Weakness in the database. The displayed 80 therefore matches Cursed Jewel's base 80 without a visible Tool, Weakness, or Stadium damage adjustment.
- The owner has no Bench at the pre-attack frame. After the Active is knocked out, the UI goes directly through the point animation to Defeat; no promotion or other forced choice appears.

## Identity boundary

The video clearly identifies Mega Sableye ex, 170 HP, Cursed Jewel 80, its exact effect text, and a two-star/full-art presentation. Eval14 contains two mechanically identical two-star prints, `B3b 081` and `B3b 088`. The collector number is not readable in the focused frames, so the exact print remains unresolved between those IDs. `B3b 041` is the mechanically identical four-diamond print and is excluded by the visible rarity presentation. Engine probes should parameterize all three reprints or use one only as an explicitly representative mechanics fixture.

## Limits

- The footage proves that the delayed effect is announced after Cursed Jewel's 80 damage and before the terminal point/result. Because the game ends immediately, it does not demonstrate the later 40-damage trigger, its expiry, or a counterattack knockout.
- The checked Auto control is visible at `227.500` and during the callout, but this review makes no optimal-play claim.
- Sparse earlier frames are consistent with the final Gabite having evolved from the previously Benched Gible during the owner's last turn; they do not support a claim that a further evolution to the Garchomp in hand was legally available.
