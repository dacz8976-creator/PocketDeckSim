## Main finding

At about **04:50-04:54**, the opponent's Benched Greninja uses **Water Shuriken** while the owner's **Regigigas is Active with Heavy Helmet**. Regigigas falls from **60 to 40 HP**, a full 20. The Helmet is present before and after the ability. Regigigas's card shows a Retreat Cost of **four Colorless**, satisfying Helmet's printed threshold of 3 or more; the game also announces the Helmet's attack reduction when the Tool is attached. Earlier, Articuno ex's printed 80-damage **Blizzard** dealt 60 to that Helmet-wearing Regigigas (120 to 60). Thus the clip directly contrasts reduction of attack damage with no reduction of Water Shuriken ability damage. The user's report about upstream simulator behavior is context only; this review did not inspect or run that code. Evidence: [Retreat Cost](evidence/regigigas_retreat4_076.jpg), [Helmet text](evidence/heavy_helmet_card_165.jpg), [Helmet application](evidence/helmet_applied_regigigas_171.jpg), [attack comparison](evidence/blizzard_helmet_277.jpg), [before Shuriken](evidence/regigigas_before_shuriken_290.jpg), [ability banner](evidence/shuriken_regigigas_292.jpg), [40 HP](evidence/regigigas_after_shuriken_294.jpg).

The Tool text shown in the clip is specific. **Heavy Helmet:** if its Pokémon has Retreat Cost 3 or more, it takes 20 less damage from attacks from the opponent's Pokémon. **Protective Poncho:** while its Pokémon is on the Bench, prevent all damage to it from the opponent's attacks and Abilities. **Clear Veil:** prevent effects of attacks by the opponent's Pokémon done to its Pokémon; existing effects remain. These are distinct scopes, so Clear Veil's text does not promise to prevent ordinary attack damage or an Ability. Evidence: [Helmet](evidence/heavy_helmet_card_165.jpg), [Poncho](evidence/poncho_card_095.jpg), [Veil](evidence/clear_veil_204.jpg).

## Boundaries

- The main claim is an observed game behavior in this exact setup: **Water Shuriken did full 20 through Heavy Helmet**. The footage alone does not establish every ability-damage interaction.
- Protective Poncho's wording covers both attacks and Abilities **while on the Bench**. Here it blocks visible Blizzard Bench damage. The clip does not show Water Shuriken targeting that Benched Poncho wearer, so its ability protection is printed-card scope rather than an observed ability-target outcome.
- Clear Veil's wording protects against **effects of attacks**, not the damage itself; the wearer takes 10 from Blizzard. No status or other non-damage attack effect is applied to the Veil wearer, so its effect prevention is not directly tested.
- No simulator source, external ruleset, or later battle result was checked in this review.
