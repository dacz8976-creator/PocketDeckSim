- First Victini: 70 -> 60 from Scyther U-turn -> 50 and 40 from two Wynaut attacks. It then retreats at 40 and stays benched. Second Victini stays 70. Neither attacks. Victory Star banners are explanatory; no actual attack-coin reroll is observed.

Its HP chain is 210 -> 160 from Doublade Slash 50 -> 150 from Wynaut 10 -> 0 from Bullet Slugger 160. Reserve Numel gains one Fire on turn 10 and does not evolve or attack.
- Owner scored knockouts: benched original Scyther 70 -> 0 for one point; active original Doublade 90 -> 0 for one point. Mega Scizor 200 -> 40 is not a knockout. Opponent has no earlier points, then takes three for Mega Camerupt ex.
- Displayed owner attack damage is 160 + 190 + 160 = 510, matching results. This includes overkill on Scyther and Doublade; it is not the sum of HP actually removed.
- Bounded Field never enters play. Rainbow Cave is manually played and used once, then opponent replaces it with Training Area. Training Area supports both Doublade's 50 and the decisive Scizor 160, as well as owner Doublade knockout 190.
- No Pokémon Tool, healing, sleep, paralysis, poison or burn is observed. Wynaut's attack-cost marker is an attack effect, not one of those conditions.

The observable loss mechanism is the third Volcanic Kaboom's random selection of the only opposing Pokémon that survives its 160 damage, followed by Mega Scizor's promotion bonus and Stadium boost. A random hit on the 30 HP Wynaut or 60 HP Honedge instead would have supplied the owner's third point from the visible board.
