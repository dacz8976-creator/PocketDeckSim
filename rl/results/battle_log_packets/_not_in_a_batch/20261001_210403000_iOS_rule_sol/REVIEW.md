2. **Victory Star rerolls an attack's effect coins, but is not offered after a failed Confusion check.** Native99-100 first shows three tails for Heat Charged;102 is the Victory Star prompt;103-104 ability activation;107-110 all three coins repeat and finish three tails;111-112 proceeds to the opponent turn with no second prompt, despite two Victini. Native311-317 and384-389 show failed condition checks without a Victory Star prompt. The printed Victini text visible at102/355 explicitly limits Victory Star to once each turn across copies.
3. **Master Plan confuses the opponent before its coin; tails also confuses the user.** Native80 shows the card;83-89 shows opponent Confused and heads with owner clear. Native281 shows its complete printed text;285 shows Bronzong Confused;287-290 gives tails and owner Confused. These are separate Trainer coins from the later condition checks.
4. **Evolving or retreating clears Confusion in the observed cases.** Native118 gives Lopunny's explicit recovered-from-Confusion message after evolving Buneary. Native412-414 gives the same message for Moltres after its retreat. Bronzong remains Confused while staying Active and fails several later checks.
