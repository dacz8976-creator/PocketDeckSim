# "Your Move, Blind": Dustin's answers (finished Sept 26)

The quiz had 33 positions, each one where k3 and kp3 choose differently. Dustin picked his move blind, without knowing which bot chose what. Quiz v1 was published on Sept 25, and v2, which adds the decklist, the next morning. The answer key and the page stayed private until he finished. Full table: `answers_table.md`.

## In plain words

- **kp3 agrees with Dustin more often than k3, and more clearly when he's sure.**
  - First move: kp3 13, k3 10, neither 10.
  - At the decision the position was built around: kp3 17, k3 13, neither 3.
    - 7 of the 10 "neither" answers began with a free Tool or Stadium play, or another move, and his own plan then sided with one bot on the decision.
  - When he marked himself "sure": kp3 5, k3 2, neither 2.
  - This is not proof. 17 against 13 is well within chance for 30 positions. But it points the same way as the table readings.
- **The clearest pattern is not about either bot's choice: Dustin plays free, no-downside Tools and Stadiums first.** He did it in 6 of 33 positions:
  - Protective Poncho, twice;
  - Deceptive Needle, twice;
  - Leaf Cape;
  - Soothing Shore.

  "Protective poncho on a bench riolu has no downsides." Neither bot made those plays first. This is the Tool blind spot (`kt`) and the Trainer-pricing class the gauntlet's variation check just flagged, now seen from the other side.
- **Copycat depends on the game state.**
  - When behind or needing specific cards, Dustin plays it (sure, 4 times): "You are 90%+ (estimate) losing this game. Not using the copycat makes your odds of losing 100%".
  - When safe and ahead, he keeps his hand: "You are safe from one hit KO next turn, copycat loses you cards".
  - kp3 plays Copycat more than k3, and the more-often-right bot here is kp3. Neither bot reasons about being behind as such.
- **Team Rocket's Boss:** twice he declined it where k3 played it. "I wouldn't use the supporter card on turn 1 because they might not even have another basic in their hand". Boss was also the card whose swap moved Suicune most in the variation check.
- **Mistakes he saw in the bots' earlier play**, which set up his positions:
  - Q29: "putting bombirdier active was a mistake";
  - Q32: "not using xspeeds for no reason, then actually using them to switch shuckle with vespiqueen…";
  - Q27: he'd have played Copycat before attaching to the benched Mega.
- **One gap in the quiz text:** Q31's description didn't mention the Burn coin at Checkup ("You didn't mention anywhere flipping a coin to see if the active was still burned").
