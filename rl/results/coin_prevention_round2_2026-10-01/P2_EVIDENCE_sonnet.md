## What the engine does today (`main` b77652d6, identical in `engine/src` on 1163ebb7; read, not run)

1. **How the effect is stored.** All five attacks add the same effect, `CardEffect::Counterattack{amount}` with duration 1, to the attacker's own Active Pokémon after its damage (`effect_mechanic_map.rs:529` Shell Trap 20, `:538` Bristling Spikes 30, `:547` Cursed Jewel and Spike Armor 40, `:2540` Needle Lariat 80; `apply_attack_action.rs:3613-3632`). Duration 1 means "during the opponent's next turn": `end_turn_maintenance` runs for both players at every turn change (`played_card.rs:340-346, 460-469`, `state/mod.rs:942-950, 1210`). Tests pin the duration (`video_evidence/mega_sableye.rs:104-118`, `alolan_sandslash_spike_armor_test.rs:72-125`).
2. **Order inside one attack** (`attack_outcome.rs:198-282`): pre-effect, damage with all modifiers (`handle_damage_only`), Hala-style survival, then a "reaction frame" (attacker, damaged Pokémon) goes on the stack *before* the attack's own post-effect (a switch, for example), the post-effect runs, then the hit-back (`handle_attack_retaliation`), then knockouts.
3. **The hit-back is raw damage** (`apply_action_helpers.rs:614-633`): `get_counterattack_damage` (`hooks/counterattack.rs:13-36`, no Active-Spot check) and then `attacker.apply_damage(counter_damage)` at `:625`. It does not go through `modify_damage` (`hooks/core.rs:1819`, which is called only for an attack's own damage or forecasts: `apply_action_helpers.rs:498, 558`, `attack_outcome.rs:1005, 1150`, `apply_attack_action.rs:6235`), through the Disguise check (`:446`, called only from `handle_damage_only` at `:576`), or through Guts (`guts_would_flip` callers: `apply_attack_action.rs:360-369`, `apply_action.rs:782`, `attack_outcome.rs:717`). So today: no damage cut, no Disguise, no Guts flip, no Weakness (P2 adds Weakness).
4. **Rescue Scarf and Lucky Egg** both need the knocked-out Pokémon to be on the *non-attacking* side (`apply_action_helpers.rs:805-817`; `hooks/core.rs:2338, 2479-2503`, check at `:2491`). In a hit-back the knocked-out Pokémon is the attacker, so the Pokémon goes to the discard pile like any other and the Egg draws nothing.
5. **Switch to the Bench** (`apply_action_helpers.rs:960-987`, `apply_activate`): the reaction frame's references are remapped to follow the damaged Pokémon to its Bench slot (`:963-977`), then the Pokémon sent to the Bench gets `clear_status_and_effects()` (`:981`), which empties the whole effect list (`played_card.rs:378-386`, `self.effects.clear()` at `:385`). So the five attacks' effect is gone before the hit-back reads the Benched holder (they add 0), but Rocky Helmet (a Tool) and an Ability on that Pokémon are still read, because `get_counterattack_damage` does not check "in the Active Spot".
6. **That Bench behaviour is a deliberate choice.** `engine/tests/rules_repair_retaliation_timing.rs:29-70` pins that Druddigon (Rough Skin), moved to the Bench by Grapploct's Knock Back, still does 20 to the attacker ("the damaged defender, not its Active replacement, owns the reaction"). Perish Body is the exception that is re-checked for the Active Spot (`attack_outcome.rs:1173-1185`, test `:136-186`), and so are knockout triggers such as Innards Out (`hooks/core.rs:2382-2384`; benched case pinned by `pokemon/pyukumuku_innards_out_test.rs:92-125`). No test covers Rocky Helmet from the Bench, and none covers a lethal push-out on a Pyukumuku.
7. **Knock Back** uses `apply_activate` and leaves the reaction frame on the stack until the opponent chooses the new Active (`apply_attack_action.rs:5111-5129`; stack asserted in `promotion_continuation_tests.rs:462-502`).
8. **Devolve** (`devolve_opponent_active`, `apply_attack_action.rs:6621-6659`): does nothing if the holder was already Knocked Out by the damage or has no cards underneath; otherwise it builds a *fresh* `PlayedCard` from the lower stage (`:6649`; empty effect list, `played_card.rs:113`) and copies only damage, Energy, Tools and the cards underneath (`:6650-6653`). So the five attacks' hit-back is lost, a Rocky Helmet still fires (Tool copied), and an Ability only if the lower stage has it. Evolving works the same way in reverse (`apply_action.rs:1567-1606`, "This removes status conditions"). Only Alolan Sandslash (Stage 1) and Chesnaught (Stage 2) among the five holders are evolved. Existing Celebi tests have no hit-back holder (`celebi_temporal_leaves_test.rs`).
9. **Hala** is registered for the player who played it and rescues only that player's Hariyama or Crabominable (`apply_trainer_action.rs:180, 1699-1710`; `apply_action_helpers.rs:916-941`). See "Hala" below for the own-turn risk.
10. **No test** has a damage cut, a Disguise, a Guts Pokémon, a Scarf or Egg holder, or a Rocky Helmet holder on the *attacking* side of a hit-back (searched `engine/tests` and `engine/src`). `ursaluna_guts_test.rs:68-109` has Guts on the holder, not on the attacker. Tests that do touch the five attacks' hit-back: Spike Armor (`alolan_sandslash_spike_armor_test.rs`), Mega Sableye (`video_evidence/mega_sableye.rs`), the bot-planning checks in `public_reply_counterattack_test.rs`.
11. **P2 on the cloud branch** (`origin/claude/coin-prevention-round2`, b7e3bc00): its own commits change four `engine/src` files (`apply_action_helpers.rs` `handle_attack_retaliation`, `hooks/core.rs` new `attack_return_weakness_extra`, `hooks/counterattack.rs` new `attack_counterattack_damage`, `hooks/mod.rs` two re-exports) and add +20 only when the attacker is the Active Pokémon, the holder still has a Counterattack effect and the attacker has a printed Weakness to the holder's type. It does not touch Rescue Scarf, Lucky Egg, Disguise, Guts, `modify_damage`, `apply_activate` or Hala. After a Knock Back or devolve the holder has no Counterattack, so P2's extra is 0 there too. (The raw `git diff main origin/claude/coin-prevention-round2 -- engine/src` is 12 files because the branch also carries earlier rounds; none of those hunks touch the retaliation path.)
12. **Bot side.** `players/value_functions.rs:1936` and `:5038` call `get_counterattack_damage`, so a rules change here would move the kd/kt valuations. Not checked further.

### Hala

By the text, Hala covers "your opponent's next turn", so the hit-back (the attacker's own turn) can never meet it. **Separate engine risk, read only, no test:** Hala is registered with duration 1, which `add_turn_effect` applies to the current turn *and* the next turn (`state/mod.rs:1055-1067`), and the rescue (`apply_action_helpers.rs:916-941`) checks neither whose turn it is nor who attacked. So on the turn the Hala player plays Hala, if their own Hariyama or Crabominable attacks into a hit-back (the five attacks, Rocky Helmet, Rough Skin and the like) that drops it to 0 HP, the knockout pass after the hit-back would leave it on 10 HP instead of Knocking it Out. The three Hala tests only cover the opponent's turn. This is an engine-versus-text risk, not a rules question; a recording only matters if Dustin ever plays Hala and then attacks into a hit-back. The earlier audit note (`rules/_research_notes/audit_2026-09-22/trainers.md:294`, "matches") did not look at the own-turn case.
