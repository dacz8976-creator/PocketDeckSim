## 1. Result

- **Every hunk of B that a game with no coin-Ability Pokémon can reach is behaviour-preserving**, and I found no hunk that changes a rule off the gate. The off-gate literal of each rewritten queued choice is the old literal field for field (section 2, rows 12 to 22). The per-thread cut reads 0 and is never written off the gate (rows 1 to 7). The one line that changes for a *full* prevention, the `retain` (row 10), drops exactly the entries the old code dropped.
- **"Off the gate" is one predicate,** `coin_damage_prevention(state, pokemon).is_some()` (`apply_attack_action.rs:264`). It reads `get_effective_card_effects(state)` (`played_card.rs:364`: the Pokémon's stored effects plus the effect derived from its in-play Ability mechanic, both pure reads). The two effects it looks for, `CoinFlipToPreventIncomingDamage` and `CoinFlipToReduceIncomingDamage`, are created in exactly one place, `card_effect_from_ability_mechanic` (`effect_ability_mechanic_map.rs:1004-1007`, from the Ability mechanics); the only `add_effect` of one is in a `#[cfg(test)]` test (`apply_attack_action.rs:7378`). So the predicate is "a Pokémon with one of the five printings' Abilities is in play, and its Ability is not switched off". The five are Bastiodon A2 114, Hisuian Goodra B3b 050, Togekiss A4 080 and Meowth B2 124 and B2 204 (the census in `coin_prevention_repair_2026-09-30/HELPERS.md` and my S-read of Sept 30: no list under `decks/` has any).
- **The list of rewritten sites is complete.** B's diff removes exactly 8 `ApplyDamage` literals (7 in `apply_attack_action.rs`, 1 in `apply_action.rs`) and adds exactly 2 (row 12's else branch and row 13's fall-through); `queued_attack_damage_choice` has 7 helper call sites plus Chase Order's (rows 14 to 20, 13). Of the 9 places where the old file builds `ApplyDamage { attacking_ref: (action.actor, 0), .. }`, 7 are these helpers and the other two are untouched (`also_choice_own_pokemon_damage`, which damages only the attacker's own Pokémon, and `shuffle_opponent_tools_into_deck_before_damage`, which HELPERS.md lists among the six sites that still skip the coin).
- **What the readings do not prove:** that the lines are *reached* by the table. That is condition 3 of the refactor rule and it comes from the watch build's off-gate counters (section 4), not from reading.
- Repair A needs no such table: no new line of it runs without a Victini (section 5).

### F5: what each exact counter means and how it is detected (all on the board, from the scan's tick)

**Every exact counter is recorded as `{"n", "first", "ticks"}`** (second read): `ticks` lists every tick at which it fired, `n` is the number of such ticks (a counter fires once per tick), `first` the first. A reader can now ask whether a counter fired in a given turn or at a given tick, which the tightened mechanic check needs (section 6, F6). Ticks are 0-based `play_tick` numbers, the numbering of `vs_trace.rs`.

### F6: the 13 Victory Star games the repairs change (`victory_star_repair_2026-09-30/smoke/rerun_R/first_diff_output.txt`), under the tightened rule

**The rule** (second read, `tightened_rule.py`; both smokes use it): the traces now carry a hash of the whole `State` before every tick, so the first difference is classified by what differs. **"On the board"** only if an exact counter fired at a tick at or before the first differing tick *in the same turn as it* (or at the cause tick, the move before it: a counter read from the chosen move fires there, and an attack that ends the turn puts it in the turn before; counters that fire after the first difference in its turn are listed and never explain it). Otherwise a difference with the same state and the same offered moves (two or more), only a different choice, is "lookahead" and needs the probe to find the gate inside kog3's three plies at that tick. A difference of the offered moves on the same state, a forced move (n = 1) that differs, a state that differs after identical moves, or a game that is a prefix of the other is a **board** difference: without a counter it is UNEXPLAINED, which stops the switch. (My reading of "the same turn as the first difference", **accepted by the coordinator on Oct 1**: the window runs up to the first differing tick, and the cause tick is added because the literal reading would have rejected a counter read from the chosen move whenever the move ends the turn. In both smokes the two readings give the same verdicts: every counter that explains a game is in the first differing tick's own turn.)
