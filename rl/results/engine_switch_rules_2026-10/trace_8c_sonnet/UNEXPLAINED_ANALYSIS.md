# Step 8c: the 7 unexplained games and the 1 judgment game, taken apart (Sonnet, Oct 1)

Decision this informs: whether the 7 unexplained games stop the rules switch (PLAN.md step 8c), and whether the judgment game is one.

## Summary

- **The five lookahead games and the judgment game have one cause, shown by an experiment, not argued.** At the first differing tick, R's search prices one candidate move through a queued coin-target damage frame that the old engine's plain `ApplyDamage` frame left unpriced at the end of the search. That is repair B(a) (damage to a coin-Ability Pokémon now flips the coin, even for a snipe's queued choice) doing what it was written to do, reaching the bot's search through the one door the probe cannot see.
  - **The test:** the same R program with only that queued form switched back to `ApplyDamage` for that one decision (the `R-revert` run) gives the old engine's score for every candidate, and the old engine's choice, in all six games. Nothing else in R changes a score. So the forecast checks (`with_heads_coin_cuts`, `expected_damage_to`, `modify_damage`), a sampled draw in the search, and the full-prevention retain/filter_map change are all ruled out for these six; they are still on in `R-revert`.
  - **The gate, counted by the bot's own search:** an instrumented search (print only) entered a pure queued attack-damage frame with a coin Ability target at `depth_left=0` in all five lookahead games, under the candidate whose score differs and under no other. In the judgment game the queued choice is applied at ply 3 (the search prices both coin branches, 50/50), inside the tree.
  - **Why `coin_probe` said "not found" (or "at the leaf"):** it follows only the mover's own consecutive moves, so it stops at the turn boundary; these five decisions are a Promote after the opponent's knockout, and the bots' search goes Promote, the forced EndTurn (free), DrawCard, an attack, and then the pure frame resolves for free. For the judgment game the probe counts `ChooseRandomEvolutionTarget` (a free pure frame in the bots' count) as a ply, which moves the choice from "applied at ply 3" to "offered at the leaf".
- **The two prefix games are repair A on the board** (the extra tick is the Victory Star offer after a Confusion heads, and `vs_confused_choice_offered/_chosen` fire at exactly that tick), and are UNEXPLAINED only because the rule says every prefix difference is (PLAN.md:136).
- **None of the eight is a defect found in R.** What is open is a rule question for the coordinator and Dustin: five games the probe cannot see, one the probe miscounts, two the rule excludes by its own wording. I have not reclassified any of them; the verdicts in `verdicts.tsv` are as the rule gave them.
- 1,391 games are "lookahead, both halves hold" because the probe **found** the gate in the mover's own moves, which is a subset of the bot's search; the blind spot can only make the probe miss, never find falsely, so those verdicts stand.
