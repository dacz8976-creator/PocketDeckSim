--- 9a35f54 ---
kpr<N>: kp plus projected readiness for the Active, in the online score and the clock (spec registered before any kpr table)

Spec, registered here before any kpr table (Dustin's option 2; not tuned). The source is docs/REVIEW_2026-09-24_direction.md on
main ("Proposed B5 fix for the Hyper Ray blind spot") and the discard-attack census.

1. Base and code. kpr<N> = kp<N> (k's blind search, PublicPricingPlayer with the 62 audited texts) with one evaluator
   change. Player code "kpr<N>", parsed before "kp<N>". Value function public_clock_effect_kpr_value_function
   (EvalFeatures::KPR). kq's and kd's features are off.

2. The change. Wherever k reads how ready the Active is, kpr reads the Active as it will stand at its next attack
   (at_next_attack): a copy with projected_active_energy attached. Two places, both sides, public information only:
   (a) k's Active online score (weight 500): the same formula (yardstick attack, damage estimate, ratio), taken on
       that copy;
   (b) the damage-aware threat clock's missing-Energy count, for the Active's candidate attacks (its own and its
       evolution forms'). The damage estimate and everything else in the clock are unchanged.

3. When is the next attack? This turn if the owner is to move and hasn't attacked, else the owner's next turn.

4. The Energy counted, from public sources the owner controls (projected_active_energy):
   - The turn's attach from the Energy Zone, of the visible type: `current` this turn if still unused, `next` for the
     next turn. None if the Zone shows no type, or if a NoEnergyFromZoneToActive turn effect covers that turn
     (State::get_turn_effects, added).
   - Abilities that attach Energy to the Active, each once, keyed on mechanic. An Ability already used this turn counts
     only for the next turn. The mechanics:
     - AttachEnergyFromZoneToActiveTypedPokemon (Ice Maker), to an Active of its type;
     - AttachEnergyFromZoneToYourTypedPokemon, AttachEnergyFromZoneToSelf and AttachEnergyFromZoneToSelfAndDamage
       (Roar in Unison), when the Active holds them (the typed one only if the Active is of its type);
     - AttachEnergyFromDiscardToSelfAndDamage (Combust), when the Active holds it and that Energy is in the discard
       pile;
     - AttachEnergyFromDiscardToActiveTypedFromBench (Dragon's Blessing), from the Bench to an Active of its type, with
       an Energy of the Active's type from the discard pile if there is one, else the first there.
     The Zone Abilities are blocked with the turn's attach.
   - Not counted:
     - AttachEnergyFromZoneToSelfAndEndTurn (no attack follows it);
     - one-off Abilities (on evolving, end of the first turn);
     - anything feeding the Bench;
     - Roar in Unison's 30 self-damage.

5. Active only. Benched Pokemon keep current readiness, so the turn's single attach is counted once, and only for the
   Active. The online-count and Energy-distance terms are unchanged.

6. Why the clock too (option 2 over option 1), written down as Dustin asked. In the clean position the spec was built
   for (Hydreigon with [D][D][D] v Suicune ex, 140 HP, no knockout; Bulbasaur benched; a [D] showing next), the
   Active score alone gives back the 500 of readiness. But the clock still counts three turns of missing Energy, so
   the owner's win goes from 3 turns to 5 (-200), and the Active-score-only version declines Hyper Ray just as kp does
   (686 to end the turn against 491 to attack). A candidate whose table can't measure the thing it was built for isn't
   worth the laptop time. With the clock projected too, the owner's win after Hyper Ray is 2 turns against 3, and
   kpr3 chips; the get_player test pins it.

7. Footprint (census, rl/results/discard_attack_census_2026-09-25 on main):
   - Energy-discard attacks: Hyper Ray (Hydreigon, all of [D][D][D]); Mega Burning (Mega Blaziken ex, one [R] of two;

--- 0adfeb7 ---
kpr<N>: the reviewers' fixes, registered as amendments to the kpr spec before any kpr table

Two reviews of 9a35f54 (rules fidelity; identity, hidden information and tests) found no blocker. They found a timing
gap in the spec, two ways the clock could misread, a double count and a dead branch in Dragon's Blessing, and tests
that couldn't fail. The spec's intent is unchanged: the Active is priced as it will stand at its next attack. These
amend how that is computed, each with its reason, before any kpr table is run.

Amendments to the spec (9a35f54's numbering):

3. When is the next attack? (was: this turn if the owner is to move and hasn't attacked, else next turn)
   Now: while the owner's turn can still bring Energy or an attack, this turn's unused sources count and so do next
   turn's; once it can't, next turn's only. For any one attack this is the same as the reviewer's fix, "this turn if
   this turn's sources pay for it, else next turn with both".
   - Reason: under the old rule the same board scored differently mid-turn and after the turn ended, on the owner's
     side. Example: Mega Sceptile ex holding [G], this turn's attach made, [G] next: mid-turn 1 of 2, after EndTurn
     2 of 2 and a turn faster. That is +350 for ending the turn inside the search (which runs without a consistent
     horizon) over continuing to develop. k has no such step. Now both read 2 of 2. A source left unused at the end
     of the turn is still lost, which is right.
   - "The turn is over" (owner_turn_is_over) now also covers the states that are really past the turn: a pending
     end, an attack used, a forced EndTurn on the stack (Giratina ex's Ability, the Supporter that attaches and ends
     the turn), a deferred or paused Checkup, and an end-of-turn evolution. Before, a deferred Checkup (every EndTurn
     against an Active Caterpie, in the Sceptile list) read as "this turn".
   - Next turn is turn_count + 2 while the owner is to move, else turn_count + 1, as before. Nothing is projected
     during setup (turn 0).
   - What is left: any "next attack" horizon has a step somewhere. Now it is at the start of the owner's turn, where
     the horizon gains the owner's following turn. In the searching player's own search, that is the opponent's turn
     start right after the searcher's EndTurn; the opponent's next Zone type is unknown there, so only its Abilities
     count a second time, and only where one round doesn't pay its attack. In the yardstick decks that is the Suicune
     list's Chien-Pao ex emptied by Diving Icicles with one Baxcalibur in play: 2 of 3 before its turn, 3 of 3 after
     (its real next attack is two turns off either way). Hydreigon's Roar plus the turn's [D] already pays Hyper Ray.
     The old step was on the searcher's own side at its EndTurn, for every deck.

2(b). The clock. (was: the Active's missing Energy projected; damage unchanged)
   Now: the clock takes the faster of the clock with and without the projection, and for the Active it projects the
   damage estimate as well as the missing Energy (its own attacks and its evolution forms').
   - Reason for the minimum: projecting only the Active can make the owner's win slower. The threat is picked by
     fewest missing Energy, so a weak Active one attach short displaced a stronger benched attacker also one short.
     Example: Deino (Headbutt 20) Active and Mega Absol ex (80) benched with [D], [D] next, v Suicune ex: k 3 turns,
     the projection alone 7. Extra Energy must never slow a clock.
   - Reason for the damage: "as it will stand at its next attack" includes damage that depends on Energy. Mega
     Lucario ex with [F][F] and [F] next does 140 at its next attack, not 90: 1 hit on Suicune ex, not 2. The Active
     score already estimated on the projected copy, so the two now agree.

4. The sources.
   - Discard-pile sources take each discarded Energy once (a local copy of the discard pile). Reason: two Dragonair
     each took the same single discarded Energy (Dustin's 11 runs two).
   - Dragon's Blessing takes the discarded type that leaves the Active's attacks fewest missing Energy (the lowest for
     any one attack, then the total), ties to the one discarded first, after the turn's other sources. Reason: the
     old rule preferred the Active's own type, which never matches (a Dragon Active; there is no Dragon Energy), so it
     always took the oldest discard. Haxorus holding [M], discard [M, F], [M] next: now [F], ready.
   - An Ability that damages its holder (Roar in Unison 30, Combust 20) isn't counted once the damage it and earlier
     ones do would knock the Active out. Reason: an attach that knocks the Active out can't lead to its attack.
   - Known limits, written down, no yardstick deck affected: an attach that puts the Active to sleep (Snoozing Habit,
     Comatose, Stellar Cradle) still counts; Energy moved from the Bench isn't counted; and a `next` drawn inside the
     search (Rainbow Cave, or a search crossing into the owner's next turn) is a guess, which matters only for decks
     with two Energy types (Dustin's 08 and 11), fix before kpr pilots one.
