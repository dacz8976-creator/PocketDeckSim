The draft's mechanism claim is sound: kp3 picks its opening Active by the static setup score alone (500 for a free attack, plus HP over knockout points, minus Retreat), the search never reaches its own first turn, and neither Boosted Evolution nor Bad Dreams is visible to that score; every number in the Altaria table was re-derived from the printed cards and the code's constants and matches, and the kp3 probes fit the rule in all 235 recorded games (recounted).
