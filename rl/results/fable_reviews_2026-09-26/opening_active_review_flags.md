Six other Active-only abilities pay from turn 1 onward (Meloetta's, Suicune ex's and Entei ex's, Caterpie's, the two Checkup ones); the draft leaves them out on purpose, but the class function will be written from this sentence.
  2. The phrase test in the draft's section 4 fails as written on three map entries; register the exact phrases and two exceptions, not one.
  3. Clause (d) with Altaria as the archetype is one test, not two: the "archetype" rows are the same rows as the "own side" rows. - **On Fable's provisional call (A alone, reserve route, Altaria as (d)): the numbers hold, the collapse of (d) is real, and the ruling is Dustin's.** kob and kor as diagnostics first is right; the split of B into its Bench-only part (Hydreigon only on the table) and its anywhere part (Altaria, Vespiquen, Weezing) can be read from kob's own rows without a new code.

The draft's "156 entries, 124 variants" (README 110) is exact.

**Flag settings.** Counting a variant once per class its own fields give (the README's rule, line 114), there are 130 settings: 124 fixed + 6 extra from `HealOneYourPokemon` (2), `SwitchOutOpponentActiveToBench` (2), `NoRetreatCost` (3 classes), `RandomEvolutionFromDeck` (2), `ReduceAttackCost` (2), `DrawCardsOncePerTurn` (only `require_active: true` is mapped, 1). **Switch A.** The only Active-only flag whose payoff exists only on the owner's first turn (or the turn played) is `CanEvolveOnFirstTurnIfActive` (`mechanic.rs` 549; map line 36-39). Passive: `move_generation_abilities.rs` 228. Printings, from `python lib/card.py "B1 184"`, `"P-B 011"`, `"P-B 054"` and a scan of `lib/deckgym-database.json` for the text: Eevee B1 184, P-B 011, P-B 054, all 50 HP, retreat 1, "As long as this Pokémon is in the Active Spot, it can evolve during your first turn or the turn you play it." No other card carries the text. The other "first turn" texts in the database are Wimpod A3 021 (`NoRetreatCost { YourFirstTurn }`, anywhere, self), Zeraora (`EndFirstTurnAttachEnergyToSelf`, anywhere, self) and Eevee ex's prohibition (`CanEvolveIntoEeveeEvolution`).

**Switch B, Bench-only (8).** Gate `!is_active` / `in_play_index != 0`: `SwitchThisBenchWithActive` (`move_generation_abilities.rs` 96), `AttachEnergyFromDiscardToActiveTypedFromBench` (265-273), `MoveFixedDamageFromActiveToThisBenched` (242-244, 290-301), `DiscardOpponentActiveToolsAndDiscardSelf` (150-152, 379-387). **Switch B, anywhere and board-scoped (44).** Same 44 as the README. **Not B, and rightly:** the 31 self-scoped anywhere flags (Shuckle ex, Arceus ex, Giratina ex, Oricorio in scope), the 12 on-evolve triggers (never on a Basic), the 4 on-Bench-entry triggers (no list in scope carries one; an engine rules question the draft records). **The "one exhaustive match, no wildcard" claim (draft 59-60).** Sound. Rust refuses a non-exhaustive `match` (E0004); the enum is not `#[non_exhaustive]` and the players' code is in the same crate; the engine already keeps `can_use_ability_by_mechanic` exhaustive (`move_generation_abilities.rs` 63-287, no `_` arm). Caveats the registration should state: (a) the four nested enums `NoRetreatCostTarget` (`mechanic.rs` 63), `NoRetreatCostCondition` (76), `AttackCostReductionScope` (100), `RandomEvolutionTrigger` (726) need wildcard-free matches too; (b) a `{ .. }` pattern on a field-dependent variant hides a new field; (c) the compile error forces *a* classification, not a correct one, and the phrase test cannot check a passive whose position lives in a hook (Bad Dreams, More Poison, Soothing Wind, Time Recall, Nightmare Aura print no position phrase), so each such arm should carry its hook's file:line, as README section 3 already does for most.

## 3. Findings

Severity: high = a claim that is false or a registration that could pass while wrong; medium = a gap the registration must close; low = wording.

1. **Medium. Class A's definition says "pays on its owner's first turn"; it must say "only".** Draft 44 ("works only from the Active Spot and pays on its owner's first turn"), README 16 ("exactly one flag"). Under the words as written, six Active-only map entries qualify (section 1 above: Meloetta's, Legendary Pulse, Full-Mouth Manner, Quick Growth, the two Checkup flags); `census.py` 39 defines `first` as "only matters during its owner's first turn", which is what the count of 1 uses. The class function will be written from the draft's sentence. Fix: "an Ability that works only from the Active Spot and whose payoff is confined to the owner's first turn (or the turn the holder is played); today exactly `CanEvolveOnFirstTurnIfActive`. Active-only Abilities that pay from the first turn onward are class C (section 9) and are left out." Use the same sentence as the doc comment on `opening_ability_class`.
2. **Medium. The phrase test in draft 96 fails as written on three map entries.** Run against the map with the draft's phrases ("in the Active Spot", "on your Bench", "first turn"): map lines 524 and 722 (`AttachEnergyFromZoneToActiveTypedPokemon`, "attach it to the [P]/[W] Pokémon in the Active Spot", the phrase names the target, class anywhere) and 782 (`CannotAttackWithoutBenchedNames`, "on your Bench" names the Regis; class Active with no Active phrase) fail, plus the stated exception 567. With the README checker's phrases ("is in the Active Spot", "is on your Bench", README 298) only 567 and 782 remain. Fix: register the phrases as "this Pokémon is in the Active Spot" / "this Pokémon is on your Bench" / "first turn", and two exceptions: `CanEvolveIntoEeveeEvolution` (a prohibition) and `CannotAttackWithoutBenchedNames` (a drawback classed Active because only the Active attacks). Add that flags with no position phrase are classed by their hook, file:line beside the arm.
3. **Medium. Clause (d) with Altaria as the archetype is one test, not two, and the fallback is not pre-stated.** Draft 201-220, esp. 210 ("both halves of (d) are read on the same rows"); REVIEW 130 clause (d) ("as a requirement and not a list entry", written for an archetype "the eight lists cannot" see); REVIEW 160, ruling 1(ii) pending. Under switch A the only carriers in scope are Altaria (and its variant lists) and Dustin's deck 15, so the "top-30 archetype not Dustin's that carries the card" is the deck the candidate was found on, and (d)'s second half adds no evidence beyond the own-side gain. The numbers support the literal reading (rank 2, not Dustin's, 99.7% carriers per `limitless_carriers.md`, which I did not re-count); the ruling is Dustin's. Fix: (i) say in (d) that under this choice the clause reduces to the own-side test and that the seven "before/after" cells are the only added evidence, reported not gated; (ii) pre-state the outcome if Dustin rules no: Mega Altaria ex Igglybuff at 4 of 25 lists, one copy each, no top-8 and no built list is an occasional carrier, so by the closure sentence the route is closed and adoption needs Dustin's explicit override (draft 213 leaves this as "the same open reading"); (iii) optional, not a clause: koa v kp3 mixed rows on the two Altaria variant lists on file (A changes 24.0% and 16.0% of their openings) as a same-archetype, different-list check.
4. **Low. The exhaustive-match sentence (draft 59) needs its caveats.** Nested enums (`mechanic.rs` 63, 76, 100, 726) must also be matched without `_`; no `..` on the five field-dependent variants; the compile error forces a classification, not a correct one. Fix: one sentence listing the four nested enums and "no wildcard at any level"; optionally `#![deny(clippy::wildcard_enum_match_arm)]` on the module.
5. **Low. README 121 "Carried by Eevee B1 184 only."** Three printings carry it (B1 184, P-B 011, P-B 054; draft 50 has them). Fix: "carried by Eevee only (B1 184, P-B 011, P-B 054); in the 42 lists only B1 184 appears."
6. **Low. README 114 never states the total the class counts add to.** Fix: "130 flag settings over 124 variants (156 map entries)".
7. **Low. "Bench-working" (draft 51) over-states the mechanism for 44 of B's 52 settings.** Only the 8 Bench-only flags work better from the Bench; the 44 work equally from the Active Spot (gates and hooks in section 1), so −250 on them says "has an ungated board Ability", not "better benched". On the table the Bench-only part touches Hydreigon only (Bombirdier), the anywhere part Altaria, Vespiquen and Weezing. Fix: in section 6's kob predictions mark Hydreigon as the Bench-only carrier and the other three as anywhere-board, so kob's rows attribute the two parts; one sentence at 51: "works from the Bench, which is not the same as better there".
8. **Low. An Active with an unmapped Ability text gets no flag.** `get_in_play_ability_mechanic` returns `None` for a text not in the map (`effect_ability_mechanic_map.rs` 878-893). None of the 48 lists' Basics has one today. Fix: one sentence in section 9, and have the coverage flag name it.
9. **Low. Clause (a) is measured, not predicted.** Draft 180 says "measured on the table"; section 5 could add that the measured share decides (a), that koa's 790 to 890 is safely under 2,100, and that kob's 14.55% straddles the line (about 6% chance of landing over), which matters only if B is ever registered.
