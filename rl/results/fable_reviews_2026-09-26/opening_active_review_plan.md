# Opening-Active draft (koa): the registration read against the plan (Fable, Sept 26, about 06:20)

**In plain words.** The draft is close to a registration and much better than the kt spec was at the same stage: it names one candidate (`koa`), defines the footprint the plan's way and estimates it before any game (6.0%), names the (d) archetype and its list source with the carrier count done (335 of 336 Espeon lists), pins the weights, reuses the table's deals, and writes down what would refute it. Five things still have to change before it is committed as a registration, two of them because a registration written as it stands could pass while wrong: the baseline is not pinned to one engine commit while repairs that reach table games (one of them Bad Dreams, Altaria's own card) are queued; and the fallback "ordinary rule" is stated wrongly (the plan's adoption rule has no "double to 2,000 when undecided" clause and no "undecided" outcome, only adopted or not adopted). The rest are gaps to close: what each answer to the panel-deck ruling does before any game, what the kob/kor rows can and cannot say about the Darkrai half, two directions the census's own class table already implies (kor on Suicune down, kob on Hydreigon up), what confirmation on post-freeze events means for a no-harm candidate, and what happens if kt is adopted first. Fable's provisional call (A alone by the reserve route, Altaria as the (d) archetype, kob/kor as diagnostics) does not loosen any fixed rule: the 15% trigger, the five clauses and the closure sentence are unchanged; the route follows from the measured footprint; the panel-deck question is a reading the text did not anticipate and is put to Dustin as a ruling, not assumed. The one place the call needs care is that with the panel's Altaria as (d), clause (d) adds nothing beyond the table's own Altaria cells; a cheap way to keep (d) a real test under either ruling is proposed below.

## Findings, by severity

### High

**1. The baseline is not pinned, and the queued engine repairs would move it.** Draft line 32: "kp3 at the official engine's commit (7fc6ccb, `rl/engine-2026-09-25/`) or current main, as the builder states." Today main's `engine/src` has no commit after 7fc6ccb (`git log 7fc6ccb..HEAD -- engine/src` is empty), so the two are the same this morning, but section 8's morning list (line 166) queues repairs for the cloud that reach table games: Legendary Pulse v Hiking Trail, Heavy Helmet at the current Retreat Cost, Rare Candy under Primeval Law, Disguise, **Bad Dreams stopped by by-attacks protections**, and the promotion-after-end-of-turn-knockout timing. The census README already notes the Bad Dreams one (README line 320) but the draft's base clause does not. If any lands before koa's table: (i) section 4's "k3 and kp3 replay all 14,000 table games" (line 84) fails by construction against the references (`rl/results/engine_identity_2026-09-25/kp3_500.jsonl`, k3 and kp3 identical 14,000 of 14,000 at 7fc6ccb); (ii) the paired Altaria cells and the (d) pooled gain would carry the Bad Dreams repair's effect on Altaria inside "koa's" change, since Darkrai is Altaria's own card in 24% of its openings; (iii) the "before" cells in the (b) table (lines 185 to 195) would be at the old engine. That is a registration that can pass while wrong. **Fix:** replace line 32 with one commit (7fc6ccb, or the repaired commit once it exists) and the reference files by path; add to section 4: "If the table's engine commit is not 7fc6ccb, kp3's 28 × 500 table and its mixed-row references are regenerated at that commit first, the games that differ from the 7fc6ccb files are listed and shown to lie in cells where the repaired mechanic is reached, scoreboard v2's kp3 row is re-scored on them, and koa's footprint, (b), (c) and (d) are read against those files." This is the same requirement the kt review made (its finding 4) and the plan's engine-repair rule (section 8 line 127).

**2. The fallback "ordinary rule" misstates the plan.** Lines 224 to 229: "the ordinary rule is the only route left for `koa`: paired ΔMSE on the 27-cell decision set, with the whole 95% interval below zero; vetoes under rule v2; when undecided, deals double to 2,000. Section 6 predicts it will most likely stay undecided even at 2,000. That is recorded here ... so that an 'undecided' reading is not read later as evidence against the feature." The doubling clause belongs to the τ̂ margin rule for variant screens (RUN5 "Variants and process": "τ̂ margin E = 3 with the 90% interval, doubling deals to 2,000 when undecided"; direction §8 "Variant screens", line 201), not to the adoption rule, whose outcomes are adopted (whole interval below zero) or not adopted; kp3's own interval crossing zero on v2 was "not adopted" and needed Dustin's override (RUN5 "Decisions taken Sept 25"). "Undecided" is not an adoption-rule outcome, and writing that an undecided reading "is not evidence against the feature" prepares an override in advance. The prediction itself is right and worth keeping: a uniform +3 to +7 on Altaria's six decision cells moves the mean squared miss by only about −2 to −4 points² (recomputed: 2rΣm + 6r² over 27 cells with Σm = −25.0 gives −3.6 at r = 3, −2.1 at r = 7), far inside kp3's own ±55 interval width. **Fix:** "If Dustin does not approve the reserve route, koa is read by the ordinary adoption rule as fixed (paired ΔMSE on the decision set, adopted only if the whole 95% interval is below zero, vetoes under rule v2, no doubling). Predicted outcome: not adopted, because a change confined to six decision cells cannot move ΔMSE beyond its noise at 500 or 2,000 deals (about −2 to −4 points²). Adoption would then be an override, recorded as such." Also line 256 ("A table ΔMSE that is undecided (predicted)") reads "not below zero (predicted)".
