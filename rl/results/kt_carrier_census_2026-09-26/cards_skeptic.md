# kt carrier census, step 1 skeptic: can cards.json / cards.md be refuted?

Written Sept 26, 2026 by the skeptic subagent. Read-only pass over `cards.json` and `cards.md` in this folder against the engine working tree (git HEAD 4924008 on main, confirmed with `git rev-parse`; this folder is untracked) and `lib/deckgym-database.json` (3,879 entries: 3,566 Pokémon, 313 Trainers). Card texts quoted here come from `python lib/card.py "<name or id>"` runs saved in the session scratchpad (`kt_census/cardpy_skeptic.out`); the id / text / deck cross-check is `kt_census/skeptic_check.py` and `skeptic_check2.py` with their `.out` files. No engine game was run, no Limitless file was opened, nothing outside this file and the scratch folder was written.

## Verdict in one paragraph

The card SET that clause (d) depends on (groups 1 to 3: 5 Trainers, 3 Tools, 25 Pokémon / 42 printings) is right: every card is registered by the engine as the kind stated, every printing id is in the database with that text, no printing with the same name and text is missing, and every Dustin-file placement reproduces over all 28 deck files. What needs to change is around the edges: (1) one temporary next-turn defensive effect the engine registers through the very same route is never mentioned, Steelix's Metal Defender (`NoWeakness`); (2) group 4 is inconsistent with its own inclusion rule for Rocky Helmet, leaving out the other two inputs of `get_counterattack_damage`, one of which (Mega Sableye ex) sits in two of Dustin's brews; (3) two counts in the prose are wrong (13 brew files, not 11; 42 printings, not 46). So: agrees = false, but nothing in groups 1 to 3 moves.

## (a) Is every listed card really registered as the stated kind? Yes.

**Group 1, Trainer turn effects.** `engine/src/actions/apply_trainer_action.rs` dispatches by CardId exactly the listed printings: 176 `A1a067Blue | A1a081Blue`, 177 `A4160Jasmine | A4200Jasmine`, 178 `B3151Cheren | B3192Cheren`, 179 `A3a063BeastWall`, 251 `A2a075Adaman | A2a090Adaman`. Adaman (934-944) adds `TurnEffect::ReducedDamageForType { amount: 20, energy_type: Metal, player: actor }` with duration 1; the other four go through `add_damage_reduction_for_next_turn` (1021-1037) into `TurnEffect::ReducedDamageForTarget` with duration 1: Blue 10 / AllPokemon / only_from_ex false (1043-1053), Jasmine 50 / NamedPokemon[Steelix, Skarmory ex] / false (1055-1060), Cheren 100 / NamedPokemon[Watchog, Stoutland] / true (1062-1067), Beast Wall 20 / UltraBeasts / false (1069-1079). `state/mod.rs` 1063-1079 `add_turn_effect` registers for `turn_count + 0..=duration`, so turns t and t+1. Read at `hooks/core.rs` 1189-1227 (`only_from_ex` gate at 1219, scope at 1230-1236), summed at 1370-1377, subtracted after Weakness at 1883-1885; `persistent_defender_damage`'s doc (1577-1582) leaves turn effects out. A whole-engine grep shows these two `TurnEffect` variants are constructed nowhere else. All as the census says.

**Group 2, Tools.** `hooks/core.rs` 687-713 Heavy Helmet: `has_tool(.., B1219HeavyHelmet)`, printed `retreat_cost.len() >= 3`, `20 * tool_count`, only for an opponent's active attack. 715-735 Metal Core Barrier: `50 * tool_count` when `state.pokemon_is_type(holder, Metal)`, active attacks only. 737-762 Steel Apron: `10 * tool_count` when Metal. `tools.rs` 105-110 `tool_count` compares effect TEXT, so the second Barrier printing B2b 117 (card.py: identical text to B2 148) counts. Discard at 504-519 at the end of the tool owner's opponent's turn. `persistent_defender_damage` 1613-1615 sums Helmet and Apron only. All as the census says. (One engine detail the census does not need: the Barrier's function has no `attacking_player != target_player` guard, unlike Helmet and Apron; not a census error.)

**Group 3, attack self-reductions.** A grep over all of `engine/src` finds `CardEffect::ReducedDamage` / `ReducedDamageFromEx` constructed only in the nine `effect_mechanic_map.rs` entries the census cites (146-153, 602-628, 2456-2466, 2554-2562, 2604-2612, 3387-3394) plus tests; `card_effect_from_ability_mechanic` (effect_ability_mechanic_map.rs 993-1011) never yields them, so "no Ability self-reduction" holds. An exact-text match of those nine texts against every attack in the database gives 25 names and 42 printings, identical, id for id, to `cards.json` (script: no missing name, no extra name, no id mismatch). Dispatch checked: `DamageAndCardEffect { opponent: false }` at apply_attack_action.rs 722 to `damage_and_card_effect_attack` 3467-3500, which `add_effect`s on the ACTOR's Active (3474-3486) and uses `binary_coin` when `coin_flip` (3492-3496); `SelfDiscardEnergyAndCardEffect` 636 to 2909; `SelfDiscardChosenEnergyAndCardEffect` 646 to 3706; `DamageAndMultipleCardEffects` 1239 to 5832. Read at core.rs 1147-1167, active-to-active only, `ReducedDamageFromEx` gated on `attacker_is_ex`. All as the census says.

**Group 4 evidence spot-checked and confirmed:** Poncho core.rs 1707-1715; Rocky Helmet counterattack.rs 13-36; Hala apply_trainer_action.rs 1699-1710 (dispatch 180); Clear Veil tools.rs 87-91; the attack preventions at effect_mechanic_map.rs 552-560, 970-984, 1126-1130, 1637-1645 and core.rs 1717-1734 / 1888-1894; Samurott 849-853 (core.rs 243) and Garchomp / Zoroark 279-282 (core.rs 2436-2462); every persistent-Ability family maps to the ability-map line the census cites, and the database resolves each text to exactly the cards the census lists.

## (b) Is a qualifying card missing? Two things to add, neither in groups 1 to 3.

**1. Steelix [B1a 051, B3 219], Metal Defender, is not mentioned anywhere.** card.py: `Steelix [B1a 051, B3 219] Metal Stage 1 (from Onix) HP 150 weak Fire retreat 4; [MMCC] Metal Defender 100 — During your opponent's next turn, this Pokémon has no Weakness.` The engine maps that text at effect_mechanic_map.rs 367-374 to `Mechanic::DamageAndCardEffect { opponent: false, effect: CardEffect::NoWeakness, duration: 1 }`, the same route and duration as every group-3 card, and applies it at core.rs 1468-1484 (`weakness_application_against` returns `WeaknessApplication::None`), which removes the Weakness stage (the flat +20, or Bounded Field's x2, per the doc at 1571) from the damage the holder takes on the opponent's next turn. `persistent_defender_damage` names `NoWeakness` among the temporary effects it leaves out (1578). It is not a `ReducedDamage`, so it is correctly absent from group 3, and the proposed kt hook (which sums `ReducedDamage` / `ReducedDamageFromEx` only) would not see it; but the census's sentence "the engine has exactly three kinds of such cut" and its group 4 skip it, while the census does list the same rider on Mega Steelix ex. It belongs in group 4 as considered and excluded, with the reason. Two things make it worth naming for the Lists phase: Steelix is one of Jasmine's two named targets, so every Jasmine list will carry a Steelix (printings: A4 122 Heavy Impact; B1a 051 / B3 219 Metal Defender, all named "Steelix"), and the engine's scope test is an exact name match (core.rs 1233 `names.contains(&pokemon.get_name())`), so "Mega Steelix ex" [B1a 052, B1a 080, B1a 086, B3 232] is NOT covered by Jasmine in the engine. That is an engine reading, not a rules claim. In Dustin's files: no Steelix of any printing (grep of decks/dustin and decks/brews).

**2. Group 4 lists Rocky Helmet "because kt prices it" (switch 3) but not the other two inputs of the same hook.** `get_counterattack_damage` (counterattack.rs 13-36) sums three things, and the proposed registration in `kt_spec_review.md` names all three for switch 3 ("Rocky Helmet, CardEffect::Counterattack such as Spike Armor, and CounterattackDamage abilities"). The census carries only the first. By its own rule the other two should be listed (or the rule dropped and Rocky Helmet moved to "not kt's switch 1"):
- `CardEffect::Counterattack`, `DamageAndCardEffect { opponent: false, duration: 1 }` at effect_mechanic_map.rs 525-549 and 2536-2543. Cards (card.py): Turtonator [B1 047] Shell Trap (20); Togedemaru [A3b 048, P-A 090] Bristling Spikes (30); Alolan Sandslash [A3 039] Spike Armor (40); Mega Sableye ex [B3b 041, B3b 081, B3b 088] Cursed Jewel (40); Chesnaught [B2 010] Needle Lariat (80).
- `AbilityMechanic::CounterattackDamage { amount: 20 }` at effect_ability_mechanic_map.rs 219-222, text "If this Pokémon is in the Active Spot and is damaged by an attack from your opponent's Pokémon, do 20 damage to the Attacking Pokémon." Cards (database exact text): Poliwrath [A1 061, B1 297] Counterattack; Druddigon [A1a 056] Rough Skin; Pawmot [A2b 028, P-A 054] Counterattack; Ferrothorn [A3a 052] Steel Spikes; Zangoose [A4a 065] Counterattack; Iron Jugulis [B3a 046] Automated Combat.
- In Dustin's files: Mega Sableye ex B3b 041 in `decks/brews/brew-07-hoopa-darkrai-sableye.txt` (1) and `decks/brews/brew-09-sableye-obstagoon.txt` (2). None of the Ability holders and none of the other attack cards in any deck file.
This changes nothing for clause (d) (switch 1) and nothing in groups 1 to 3; it is a completeness gap in the "considered" group.

**Confirmed complete otherwise.** The census's claim that a wider scan of every attack text for "takes -N damage" found no unmapped card is right: every attack text in the database containing "takes -N damage" is one of the nine mapped texts. The only other next-turn self texts are the vulnerability attacks (Lucario B3 080 "+20", Kommo-o A3 127 "+30", Hariyama B4 080 "+50": the opposite direction) and the Counterattack family above. Among Trainers, the only texts with "-N damage" or "prevent" are the five group-1 cards, the three group-2 Tools, Protective Poncho and Clear Veil; no Item, Stadium or Supporter adds a `CardEffect::ReducedDamage` to a Pokémon (whole-engine grep). No Ability text in the database registers a temporary reduction; the two on-trigger Abilities (Samurott's Stance, Garchomp's and Zoroark's knockout protection) are full preventions and are in group 4.

## (c) Printing ids and Dustin-file placements: right, with two wrong counts in the prose.

- Every id in `cards.json` (38 name+ids entries and every card in the family lists) exists in the database under that name with that text, and no same-name printing carrying the same text is left out (script: 0 problems). - Deck placements: the deck parser read all 28 files (15 in `decks/dustin`, 13 in `decks/brews`; 368 card lines, the 28 "Energy:" lines aside; every line's id and name agree with the database). By printing id the placements match the census exactly (0 differences), including the group 1-3 carrier summary: deck 01 (1 Heavy Helmet B1 219), deck 03 (2 Heavy Helmet B1 219), deck 05 (1 Cheren B3 151), deck 07 (2 Skarmory ex A4 124, 1 Steel Apron A4 153, 2 Metal Core Barrier B2 148, 2 Jasmine A4 160), deck 11 (1 Archaludon B4a 056); no brew.
