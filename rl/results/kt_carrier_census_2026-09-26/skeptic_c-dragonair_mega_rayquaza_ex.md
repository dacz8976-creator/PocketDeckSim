## 4. (d) Does the engine register Gouging Fire's attack as a self damage cut for the opponent's next turn?

Yes. Read from the engine source (main working tree, Sept 26):

- `engine/src/actions/effect_mechanic_map.rs:3387-3394` maps the exact database text "Discard 2 Energy from this Pokémon. During your opponent's next turn, this Pokémon takes -30 damage from attacks." to `Mechanic::SelfDiscardChosenEnergyAndCardEffect { count: 2, effect: CardEffect::ReducedDamage { amount: 30 }, duration: 1 }`. The database entry for B3a 054 (`engine/src/database.rs:70466-70480`) carries that text on Scorching Interruption, [RLC], fixed damage 100. (A grep for "Gouging" or "Scorching" in the map file finds nothing because the map is keyed by effect text, not card name; the mechanic's doc comments in `attacks/mechanic.rs:258` and `apply_attack_action.rs:3704` name the card.)
- `apply_attack_action.rs:646-657` dispatches it to `self_discard_chosen_energy_and_card_effect` (3706-3723): damage first, then a `ChooseAttackEnergyDiscard { energies, defensive_effect: Some((effect, duration)) }` choice is pushed for the attacking player.
- `apply_action.rs:834-844` resolves that choice: it discards the chosen Energy from the attacker's Active and calls `state.get_active_mut(action.actor).add_effect(effect, duration)`, so the ReducedDamage 30 lands on the attacker's own Active.
- `state/played_card.rs:340-346`: duration 1 means "during opponent's next turn"; `end_turn_maintenance` (460-468) decrements at each end of turn and drops effects at 0, so an effect added with 1 during the attacker's turn survives that turn's end and is removed at the end of the opponent's turn. That is one opponent turn, as the card says.
- `hooks/core.rs:1147-1167` (`get_reduced_card_effect_modifiers`) reads `CardEffect::ReducedDamage` on the defending Active for active-to-active hits, it is summed into the reductions at 1364-1369 and subtracted after Weakness at 1883-1885.

So the mechanic is the one `cards.md` group 3 describes, the amount is 30, the duration is the opponent's next turn, and the card is a switch-1 card. Two reading notes, neither a defect in this census file: the effect is attached only when the Energy-discard choice resolves (a separate action after the damage step), and the reduction applies to active-to-active hits only, an engine trait shared by every group-3 card and already recorded in `cards.md`.

## 5. (e) Is the "Dustin's deck" question stated fairly?

The numbers are right and both readings are attributed correctly:

- Reading 1 (conservative): `scan_census.py` `dustin_verdict` marks the archetype `shares_pokemon` because Dragonair is in the archetype name and 2 Dragonair B4 117 are in `decks/dustin/11-archaludon-haxorus-dragonair.txt`; `recount.py` `DUSTIN_MAPPED` gives the same verdict with the quoted CONSERVATIVE note. A grep of `decks/**/*.txt` for Rayquaza, Gouging, Dragonair or Dratini finds only deck 11's Dratini B4 116 and Dragonair B4 117 lines; no brew carries any of them.
- Overlap, re-derived by printing id: 5 of the list's 13 ids are in deck 11 (Dragonair B4 117 x2, Poké Ball P-A 005 x2, Professor's Research P-A 007 x2, Rainbow Cave B4 155 x2, Copycat B1 225 one of two), 9 of 20 copies in common; Dratini is the same name in another printing (A1 183 here, B4 116 in deck 11); list-only names are Gouging Fire, Mega Rayquaza ex, Cyrus, Professor Sada, Skull Fossil, Ancient Booster Energy Capsule, Small Balloon; deck-11-only names are Duraludon, Archaludon, Axew, Fraxure, Haxorus, Iris, Lucky Ice Pop; Energy lines "Fire, Lightning" versus "Fighting, Metal". All as the provenance says.
- What deck 11 is, from its file and `card.py`: Energy Fighting, Metal; Duraludon B4a 055 ([FM] Power Beam 50) into Archaludon B4a 056 ([FMCC] Protect Charge 110, "During your opponent's next turn, this Pokémon takes -30 damage from attacks."); Axew, Fraxure, Haxorus B2b 054-056 ([FM]/[FMC] attacks, Haxorus 150 HP) with Iris B2b 067 (an extra point on a Haxorus knockout); Dratini B4 116 into Dragonair B4 117 (Ability Dragon's Blessing: "Once during your turn, if this Pokémon is on your Bench, you may attach an Energy from your discard pile to your Active [N] Pokémon."); Lucky Ice Pop, 2 Poké Ball, 2 Professor's Research, 1 Copycat, 2 Rainbow Cave. Every Pokémon in it is Dragon type per `card.py`. So "a Fighting/Metal Archaludon-Haxorus deck that uses Dragonair as its energy engine" is an accurate description.

**The one thing I would change.** The reading-2 sentence in `dustin_readings` says deck 11 "uses Dragonair as its energy engine; this list is a Fire/Lightning deck whose attackers are Gouging Fire and Mega Rayquaza ex." That contrast leaves out that this list runs the same engine: 2 Dragonair B4 117 and 2 Rainbow Cave B4 155 are in both files, Dragon's Blessing attaches Energy from the discard pile to the Active [N] Pokémon in both, and every attacker in both decks is a [N] Pokémon (here Mega Burst discards its own [R] and [L] Energy, which is what Dragon's Blessing can return). Reading 1's case is that the shared Pokémon is the shared engine, not an incidental overlap, and the file should say so beside reading 2's differences so Dustin sees both at equal strength. On the other side, neither reading says that the two decks' switch-1 cards are different: deck 11's is Archaludon B4a 056 (Protect Charge, the same "-30 during your opponent's next turn" text; `cards.md` lists deck 11 under group 3 for it) and it is not in this list, while Gouging Fire is in no Dustin file. Under either reading, the card kt would price in this archetype is not one Dustin's deck carries. Suggested wording for `dustin_readings` (a note, no number changes): "Both decks run the same 2 Dragonair B4 117 + 2 Rainbow Cave B4 155 engine feeding a Dragon attacker; the attackers, Energy types and switch-1 cards differ (deck 11's group-3 card is Archaludon B4a 056, not in this list; Gouging Fire is in no Dustin file)." The census still takes no position; this only makes both readings' facts visible in one place.
