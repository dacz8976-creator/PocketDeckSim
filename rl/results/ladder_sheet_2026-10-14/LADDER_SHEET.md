### Brew 07: Hoopa ex / Darkrai ex / Mega Sableye ex (Darkness)

- **Scores:**
  - Strong bot: **79.1%** (range 72.1 to 84.6).
  - Everyday bot on the same deals: 63.1%.
  - Floor: clears, 60.8%.
- **How the strong bot opened:**
  - **Turn 1:** Poké Ball, Deceptive Needle on a Darkness Pokémon, Hoopa ex on the Bench, and attach Darkness when going second.
  - **First attack:** Hoopa ex's **Shadow Bullet** (D: 30, plus 20 to a Benched Pokémon).
    - Going second, it came on your first turn in about half the games.
    - Going first, it came on your second turn in 6 games in 10.
  - **The heavier hitters:** Mega Sableye ex's **Cursed Jewel** (DC: 80; whatever attacks it next turn takes 40 back, or 60 if weak to
    Darkness) and Darkrai ex's **Dark Prism** (DDC: 80).
- **Watch out for:**
  - Sceptile: 55% with the strong bot, 37% with the everyday bot.
  - Next hardest, Vespiquen: 68% / 50%.

  - Floor: clears, 33.1%.

    - The first Grass Knot came by your third turn in 45% of games, by your fourth in 65%, and never in about 1 game in 6.
    - Before it, Cottonee, Spinarak or Ariados chip in.

  the floor page were played that way.
- **Watch out for:**
  - Blaziken (40% strong bot / 18% everyday bot);
  - Lucario (45% / 24%);
  - Sceptile (55% / 28%).

## How to read the numbers

- **The two bots.**
  - The strong bot (kx3) is slow and careful. It plays your deck 20 games against each of the eight lists (10 shuffles, each from both
    sides), 160 in all.
  - The everyday bot (km3) plays the same deals, and plays all eight lists.
- **The range** is the luck of those 160 games. Two decks less than about 13 points apart can't be told apart:
  - brew 07 v deck 13 (79 v 71) is within luck;
  - deck 13 v deck 12 (71 v 61) is within luck;
  - brew 07 v deck 12 (79 v 61) is not.
- **The gap between the two bots** is 13 to 27 points on every deck. It mostly says the strong bot plays better, partly because it knows
  the eight lists.
- **The toughest lists** come from 20 games each, so each could be about 20 points off. The everyday bot's 240 games a list find the same
  soft spots, so they are probably not luck.
- **The eight lists** (Altaria, Blaziken, Hydreigon, Lucario, Sceptile, Suicune, Vespiquen, Weezing) are fixed public lists from before
  this season.
  - In the 55 games you had logged by Sept 28, 15 opponents played one of them or a close variant.
  - 29 played other established tournament decks, 1 a homebrew, and 10 weren't identified.
  - So most of your opponents play decks outside these eight.
- **The floor** is the everyday bot's 1,920-game check that a deck isn't hopeless.
  - It fails at about 18% or less, and is borderline up to about 22%.
  - Its percentage counts wins only.

## Playing and logging

- **About twenty games with one deck.** Your Sept 28 rule: play the one you'll enjoy twenty games of. Switching was what spread last
  season's log thin.
- **Losing streaks are normal.**
  - A deck that truly wins 60% loses three in a row somewhere in twenty games about half the time (56%), and four in a row about 1 time
    in 4.
  - Judge it on the twenty, not on a streak. Stopping early is your call.
- **Ladder Log** (https://claude.ai/artifact/GHSfGSCzNiWNj8SFPXV3R1): put your three items in the note box.
  - First or second.
  - The turn of your main attacker's first attack, counting your own turns (your first is T1): Hoopa ex, Whimsicott ex or Alolan
    Ninetales ex.
  - The opponent's points then.

  For example `2nd | Hoopa T1 | opp 0 pts`, or `Whimsicott never`.
  - Brew 07, deck 12 and deck 13 already have sections.
  - The page still counts from Sept 15, so last season's games are in its totals.
