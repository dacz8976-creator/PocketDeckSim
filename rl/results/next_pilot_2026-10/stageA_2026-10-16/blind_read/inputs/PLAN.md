# His Kingambit deck and his plan, in his own words (Dustin, Oct 15)

His list is `his_list.txt`. Card texts are in `cards.md`.

**The plan:**
- "That deck is not about getting kingambit as quickly as possible. I don't play pawniard right away because of that. You want both
  glimmora to have a turn. Starting with regigigas as a wall with the chance to use ilima to pull him back. You don't want muk/pawniard
  up front and that is the way you lose. ... A big enough blade is defined as enough to knock out their likely strongest pokemon and win
  the game."
- "Well I need time to get two energies on it which is two turns, which is how long it takes to evolve. Ideally pawniard hits the bench
  after one pokemon has been ko'ed. The deck has 5 pokemon (with evolutions), 2 glimmora, muk, Regigas and kingambit. The playfield has
  4 spots. So pawniard taking a spot doesn't help"
- "Take the knockout if you win or it helps you, but 1 point early doesn't help if it puts you in a worse position later."
- "kingambit is the way to win, so protecting pawniard is what I favor"
- "Glimmora gives up on average 0.5 points tho. So I can (on average) have 3 Pokemon knocked out and raise kingambit's attack to 180"
- "The muk helps get rid of sleep decks darkrai and ogerpon's protection, but it hurts if you pair it with any basic ability"
- On Copycat: "If I have one card that I really need, I don't use copycat for fear of losing it, but the scales tip at some point if I
  have 2 cards in my hand and the opponent has 8 cards."

**Rules that matter here** (Pokémon TCG Pocket; check any card in `cards.md`):
- 3 points win the game. A knocked-out Pokémon gives 1 point, an ex gives 2, and a Mega ex gives 3.
- Overlord's Blade does 60 plus 40 for each of his own Pokémon knocked out so far this game, whether or not the opponent got a point for
  it.
- Glimmora's Shattering Crystal: when it is knocked out, flip a coin; on heads the opponent gets no point.
- Alolan Muk switches off the abilities of Basic Pokémon in play. Regigigas's Seal of Antiquity is such an ability, so with Muk in play
  Regigigas can use Giga Turbo.
- Each player has an Active spot and 3 Bench spots, and attaches one Energy per turn from the Energy Zone.
