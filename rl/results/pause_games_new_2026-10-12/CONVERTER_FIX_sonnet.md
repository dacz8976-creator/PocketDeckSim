## Result

- **125 positions in 24 games** (the first version had 121 in 23): 215749, whose filmed QR list is the game's own deck (the live log held the other version), now converts (3 positions), and 020518's turn 13 (1).
- His cards changed in **50 positions in 12 games**; 19 more changed only in the plan; 2 only in the Energy type; **4 are new**; 50 are identical to the first version. (The review's 66 positions in 11 games count every position of the 11 games whose deck was wrong; 38 of them had a wrong card in hand or on the board.)
- **6 positions** still carry a card of his that the ledger cannot settle between two functions: 033529 t01 t03 (Aegislash), 020518 t01 (Sandslash), t15 (Conkeldurr), t17 (Sandslash, Conkeldurr), 012303 t09 (Conkeldurr). They are named in `his_cards_not_settled`. (The first full conversion had 28: the text the ledger reads on a card of the hand settles most of the rest.)
- **34 positions** carry an opponent card the ledger cannot settle (`opponent_cards_not_settled`); in 16 of them it is only a stack card under an evolved Pokémon (printed-line guess).
- Not converted: the four games with no deck list in the recording (20261004_214518, 20261004_214731, 20261005_034229, 20261006_012140), and two turns of 014541.
- All 125 positions load in the position runner with their decks without a problem (deck counts, card ids, the `effects`, including `NoAbilities`): a load check with km3 for one seed, run again on the final files; nothing else read from it.
