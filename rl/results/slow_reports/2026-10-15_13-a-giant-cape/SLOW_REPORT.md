## What it says

Question: how does 13-a-giant-cape do when kx3 plays it against the 8 public lists, and is that better than when km3 plays the same deck on the same deals?

Size: 160 kx3 games + 160 cheap km3 baseline games (10 deals x 2 seats against each of the 8 public lists; the baseline games are the same deals with km3 on the deck).

13-a-giant-cape scored **72.5%** over **160 games** (10 deals x 2 seats against each of the 8 public lists): probably between 65.1% and 78.8% (95% interval; this range refers to these 160 kx3 games). A win counts 1, a tie 1/2, a loss 0.

### How much better kx3 plays this deck than km3

On the same deals, kx3 scored **+17.5 points** compared with km3 piloting this deck (kx3 72.5%, km3 on this deck 55.0%, over 160 paired games: each a deal and seat played by both pilots, so 160 kx3 games + 160 cheap km3 baseline games), probably between +11.3 and +23.7 points (95% interval; this range refers to those 160 paired games).

That range does not include no gain: on these 160 paired games kx3 did better than km3. It does not say by exactly how much: that part of the range is as wide as 160 paired games make it.

## What these numbers can and can't say

**These numbers say how 13-a-giant-cape did in simulated games with kx3 playing it against the 8 public lists, probably between 65.1% and 78.8%; they are not a ranking, they say nothing about decks outside those lists, and there is no pass or fail line.**

In more detail:

- kx3, a strong but slow pilot, plays your deck knowing its own cards. It does not know which of the 8 public lists it faces, but its opponent is always one of them and always plays like the program kx3 imagines when it looks ahead, so it is better informed here than it would be against a person on the ladder or a deck outside the 8 lists. Read the score as the optimistic end.
- The 8 lists count equally here, not by how common they are on the ladder.
- The range covers only the luck of the shuffles and coin flips in these 160 games; it does not cover how true to the real game the simulator is. It can tell whether kx3 plays 13-a-giant-cape clearly above or clearly below an even score against these 8 lists; it cannot tell two decks apart whose scores differ by less than the width of such a range (13.7 points here), and more deals narrow it.
- There is no pass or fail line: whether 13-a-giant-cape is worth playing is a call for the player.
