== full message of 1981bb4
kpr<N>: each side read over its own horizon (amendment 5, registered before any kpr table, Dustin's go-ahead)

A second read of 0adfeb7 found that amendment 3 (count this turn's unused sources and next turn's while the owner's
turn runs) moved the turn-boundary step from the searcher's side to the opponent's, and made it bigger than
0adfeb7's message said. Dustin chose to fix it before the table.

The finding. kp's blind search (consistent_horizon false) scores every line that ends the searcher's turn, attacks
included, at the start of the opponent's turn; lines cut at depth mid-turn are scored mid-turn. At the opponent's
turn start, 0adfeb7 read the opponent's Active through its next turn too, so its Energy Abilities counted twice.
- Suicune list, measured on the evaluator: player 1's Chien-Pao ex with no Energy and a benched Baxcalibur, [W]
  next, against Mega Lucario ex. Before the rotation, [W] and Ice Maker's [W] pay Icicle (20): clock 10. After, a
  second Ice Maker pays Diving Icicles (130): clock 2. kpr's value for the searcher drops about 800 on every line that
  ends its turn, k's doesn't move. The same for Baxcalibur as the Active with no [W] (a 267 step), and for Chien-Pao
  ex with no Energy for any reason (promoted, pulled up by Sabrina or Cyrus).
- 0adfeb7's "What is left" paragraph put this at 2 of 3 to 3 of 3 in the score. The score's yardstick had already
  moved to Icicle, so the step is in the clock, and it's larger. That paragraph is wrong; this replaces it.

Amendment 5 (spec item 3, "when is the next attack?"), per side:
- The evaluating player's own Active: through its next turn (0adfeb7's rule). While its turn runs, this turn's
  unused sources and next turn's; otherwise next turn's. For the missing Energy of any one attack this equals "this
  turn if this turn's sources pay it, else next turn with both". (0adfeb7 said "for any one attack"; it holds for
  missing Energy, not for Energy-dependent damage: Mega Lucario ex holding [F] with this turn's [F] unused and [F] next
  is priced at 140, where an attack this turn would do 90. The clock doesn't model whose turn it is, so this stands.)
- The opponent's Active: at its very next attack (9a35f54's rule, with 0adfeb7's "turn is over" markers). While its
  turn runs, this turn's sources only; otherwise next turn's.
- Reason: each reading steps once per cycle, somewhere. The own reading steps at the start of the owner's turn and the
  opponent's at the end of its turn, and the searcher's search runs from its own turn to the start of the opponent's
  (kp's blind search goes no further). So neither reading steps inside the search. At the opponent's rotation its
  `next` becomes `current`: same Energy before and after. The +350 step 0adfeb7 removed stays removed.
- In code: enum Horizon { ThroughNextTurn, NextAttack }. parametric_value_function_ex6 passes ThroughNextTurn for the
  evaluator's Active score and for its own threat in the opponent's clock, and NextAttack for the opponent's Active
  score and for the opponent's threat in the evaluator's clock. Everything else in 0adfeb7 stands.

Known limits added to the docs (from the same read; no yardstick deck is affected unless named):
- An estimate that reads the Active from the board (Energized Leaves' combined count, Teal Mask Ogerpon ex in the
  Vespiquen list) doesn't see the projected Energy.
- Extra Energy can move the online score's yardstick to a better attack with a lower ratio, only with mixed-type
  costs (Dustin's 08 and 11, not the table).
- An observation hides the opponent's stack, so a paused end of its turn (a point-denial coin at Checkup) reads as its
  turn running. Between Kiawe and its attach choice the turn reads as over.
- Correction to 0adfeb7's message: 18 kpr feature tests there, not 19 (20 with the two through get_player).

k, kp, kq and kd: unchanged path. The clock and the Active score take Option<Horizon>, None for them; the kq and kd
test helpers pass None. A full replay follows at this commit.

Tests: 20 kpr feature tests and 2 through get_player (22).
- New: the opponent's Active at its very next attack (this turn's sources only while its turn runs; both readings
  agree once it's over or on the other side's turn); the second review's Suicune board, where the opponent's clock is
  10 before and after its turn start (2 if it were read through its next turn), and kpr's difference from k is the
  same on both sides of the boundary.
- Added, for mutations the review showed would survive: Combust over two turns (each discarded [R] once, the 20s add
  up); Forest Breath and Volt Charge blocked by the turn effect; Dragon's Blessing ties go to the first discarded, and
  the total decides when the lowest ties (Ultra Necrozma ex); the evolution form's damage projected (Electabuzz
  holding [L][L][L] with Electivire in hand: 120 with the fourth [L]); a turn-end marker under another choice (Kiawe's
  [EndTurn] under its attach choice, a paused Checkup under a promotion).
- Changed: the kpr evaluator against k is +800, then +450 (the opponent to move is read at its next attack, this
  turn), as at 9a35f54.

== full message of 9a35f54
kpr<N>: kp plus projected readiness for the Active, in the online score and the clock (spec registered before any kpr table)

Spec, registered here before any kpr table (Dustin's option 2; not tuned). The source is docs/REVIEW_2026-09-24_direction.md on
main ("Proposed B5 fix for the Hyper Ray blind spot") and the discard-attack census.

1. Base and code. kpr<N> = kp<N> (k's blind search, PublicPricingPlayer with the 62 audited texts) with one evaluator
   change. Player code "kpr<N>", parsed before "kp<N>". Value function public_clock_effect_kpr_value_function
   (EvalFeatures::KPR). kq's and kd's features are off.

2. The change. Wherever k reads how ready the Active is, kpr reads the Active as it will stand at its next attack
   (at_next_attack): a copy with projected_active_energy attached. Two places, both sides, public information only:
   (a) k's Active online score (weight 500): the same formula (yardstick attack, damage estimate, ratio), taken on
       that copy;
   (b) the damage-aware threat clock's missing-Energy count, for the Active's candidate attacks (its own and its
       evolution forms'). The damage estimate and everything else in the clock are unchanged.

3. When is the next attack? This turn if the owner is to move and hasn't attacked, else the owner's next turn.

4. The Energy counted, from public sources the owner controls (projected_active_energy):
   - The turn's attach from the Energy Zone, of the visible type: `current` this turn if still unused, `next` for the
     next turn. None if the Zone shows no type, or if a NoEnergyFromZoneToActive turn effect covers that turn
     (State::get_turn_effects, added).
   - Abilities that attach Energy to the Active, each once, keyed on mechanic. An Ability already used this turn counts
     only for the next turn. The mechanics:
     - AttachEnergyFromZoneToActiveTypedPokemon (Ice Maker), to an Active of its type;
     - AttachEnergyFromZoneToYourTypedPokemon, AttachEnergyFromZoneToSelf and AttachEnergyFromZoneToSelfAndDamage
       (Roar in Unison), when the Active holds them (the typed one only if the Active is of its type);
     - AttachEnergyFromDiscardToSelfAndDamage (Combust), when the Active holds it and that Energy is in the discard
       pile;
     - AttachEnergyFromDiscardToActiveTypedFromBench (Dragon's Blessing), from the Bench to an Active of its type, with
       an Energy of the Active's type from the discard pile if there is one, else the first there.
     The Zone Abilities are blocked with the turn's attach.
   - Not counted:
     - AttachEnergyFromZoneToSelfAndEndTurn (no attack follows it);
     - one-off Abilities (on evolving, end of the first turn);
     - anything feeding the Bench;
     - Roar in Unison's 30 self-damage.

5. Active only. Benched Pokemon keep current readiness, so the turn's single attach is counted once, and only for the
   Active. The online-count and Energy-distance terms are unchanged.

6. Why the clock too (option 2 over option 1), written down as Dustin asked. In the clean position the spec was built
   for (Hydreigon with [D][D][D] v Suicune ex, 140 HP, no knockout; Bulbasaur benched; a [D] showing next), the
   Active score alone gives back the 500 of readiness. But the clock still counts three turns of missing Energy, so
   the owner's win goes from 3 turns to 5 (-200), and the Active-score-only version declines Hyper Ray just as kp does
   (686 to end the turn against 491 to attack). A candidate whose table can't measure the thing it was built for isn't
   worth the laptop time. With the clock projected too, the owner's win after Hyper Ray is 2 turns against 3, and
   kpr3 chips; the get_player test pins it.

7. Footprint (census, rl/results/discard_attack_census_2026-09-25 on main):
   - Energy-discard attacks: Hyper Ray (Hydreigon, all of [D][D][D]); Mega Burning (Mega Blaziken ex, one [R] of two;
     also Dustin's 06); Terminating Tail (Mega Sceptile ex, one [G] of two); Diving Icicles (Chien-Pao ex in the
     Suicune list, all [W]).
   - Energy sources beyond the turn's attach: Roar in Unison (Hydreigon), Ice Maker (Baxcalibur, Suicune list),
     Dragon's Blessing (Dragonair, Dustin's 11).
   - k3 declines only Hyper Ray's chip (19% use without a knockout), against 92% for Mega Burning and 95% for
     Terminating Tail.
   Broader, and stated here so the table isn't misread: because the turn's attach is projected, every Active one
   attach short now reads as ready in both places, on both sides, in every deck. That's faithful to Pocket (a Pokemon
   can attach and attack in the same turn), and it moves more than the discard decks.
