## Confinement (why these flags)
- **`--restricted`:**
  - confines the file tools to the working directory plus `--add-dir`;
  - removes the shell and the code-running tools;
  - ignores user and project settings.
- **`--bare`** skips auto-memory and CLAUDE.md discovery. It needs `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` in the environment; OAuth and keychain are not read.
- **`--permission-mode acceptEdits`** lets the worker write in its out-folder.
- **Smoke test** (Oct 25, Haiku, dummy files only, under $0.10), cwd a read-only folder with `--add-dir` of an out-folder:
  - Writing the out-folder: OK.
  - Reading a file one level up, or a sibling folder: refused.
  - Writing the in-folder: EACCES. Writing a sibling: refused.
  - No shell. No Glob or Grep, even with `--tools` naming them; the worker has Read and Edit only, so `FILES.txt` lists every path.
  - No canary leaked.
- **What this buys:**
  - Part A can't reach `inv_out_pk/` by any tool, by accident or otherwise.
  - No worker can write outside its out-folder, including v1.6's `~/exam/inv/`.
  - The workers share no auto-memory.
- **One model for all six:** `claude-sonnet-5-5`, as v1.6's coder was a Sonnet worker. Change it only for all six together.
