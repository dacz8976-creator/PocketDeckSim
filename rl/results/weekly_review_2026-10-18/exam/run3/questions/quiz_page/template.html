Your Move, Exam Questions

These spots come from your ranked games of Oct 16-24. Each one shows the board at the start of that turn, what you played, and another line someone thought might be just as good. Judge it with only what you could see at that moment, the way you would have in the game. "My play was a mistake" is a perfectly good answer, and so is "Not sure".

How each answer counts
  • The other line was better (my play was a mistake): the other line becomes the right answer for that spot.
  • Both were fine: either line counts as right.
  • My play was better: only your line counts.
  • Not sure: only your line counts, and the other line is left out.
  • A note on your thinking is optional and is kept with your answer. A question you skip leaves that spot out of the test.
