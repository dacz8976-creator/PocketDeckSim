### 41083304332057 — Grapploct Knock Away Bench Rule
**Language:** Japanese  
**URL:** https://app-ptcgp.pokemon-support.com/hc/ja/articles/41083304332057  
**Updated:** 2024-12-25  
**Title:** オトスパスのわざ「なぐりとばす」で相手のポケモンをきぜつさせたのにバトルトライをクリアできない

**Q (Verbatim):** オトスパスのわざ「なぐりとばす」で相手のポケモンをきぜつさせたのにバトルトライをクリアできない

**A (Verbatim):** バトルトライは、バトル場のポケモンを倒すことが条件となります。「なぐりとばす」は場のポケモンがダメージを受けた後、ベンチに戻ってきぜつするため、バトルトライの条件を満たしていません。

**Translation (EN):** Why can't I complete the Battle Trial when I defeat the opponent's Pokémon using Grapploct's "Knock Away"?

A: The Battle Trial requires defeating a Pokémon in the Active Spot (battle area). "Knock Away" deals damage to a Pokémon in the Active Spot, then moves it to the Bench where it faints, so it does not meet the Battle Trial's condition.

---

**Title:** Why didn't the Battle Task complete when I knocked out the opponent's Pokémon using Grapploct's "Knock Back" attack?

**Q (Verbatim):** Why didn't the Battle Task complete when I knocked out the opponent's Pokémon using Grapploct's "Knock Back" attack?

**A (Verbatim):** The Battle Task requires knocking out a Pokémon specifically in the Active Spot. "Knock Back" deals damage while the opponent's Pokémon is in the Active position but moves it to the Bench before it faints. Since the Pokémon leaves the Active Spot before being knocked out, it doesn't fulfill the task requirement of defeating an "Active Pokémon."
