## 57286049312665 How do I calculate damage for Pokémon that have Weakness or effects applied?
"Follow these steps to calculate damage:"
1 "Start with the attack's damage."
2 "Apply any effects that affect the Attacking Pokémon."
3 "Apply Weakness."
4 "Apply any effects that affect the Defending Pokémon."
"For example, when applying Weakness for a 50-damage attack against an opponent's Active Pokémon that has an effect stating "This Pokémon takes –20 damage from attacks" with the Stadium card Bounded Field in play, the opponent's Active Pokémon takes 80 damage."
"50 × 2 – 20 = 80"
(No sentence about floor at 0, Bench, or classification of effects.)

## 57285887108377 If both players have cards in play with effects that happen at the end of each turn, in what order are the effects applied?
"If both players have cards in play with effects that happen at the end of each turn, the effects of the cards belonging to the player whose turn is ending are applied first."
Example: Darkrai (Mega Shine) Bad Dreams vs an Asleep Active with Lum Berry.
- Darkrai player's turn ends: Bad Dreams first (20 to opp Active if Asleep), then Lum Berry cures Sleep, Lum Berry discarded.
- Lum Berry player's turn ends: Lum Berry cures Sleep first, Lum Berry discarded; Bad Dreams then does nothing (not Asleep).

## 61529839106073 About the Effect of the Item Card Team Rocket's Thieving Machine
"A card that you put into your hand from your opponent's discard pile using the effect of the Item card Team Rocket's Thieving Machine is treated as your card for the rest of that battle."
"After the battle, the card is returned to its original owner."
Lucky Ice Pop taken this way: "If heads, that Lucky Ice Pop returns to your hand. If tails, it is put into your discard pile."
"If you use the Supporter card Copycat while you have an Item card in your hand from your opponent's discard pile, shuffle your entire hand, including that Item card, into your deck."

## 59513864093209 Why couldn't I choose a Pokémon in play with Wallace's effect?
"Wallace's effect allows you to choose one of your Water-type Pokémon in play with a maximum HP of 50."
"If your Pokémon's maximum HP increases to more than 50, such as with a card effect, you can no longer choose it for Wallace's effect."
Example: 50-HP Staryu + Giant Cape (+20) = 70 max HP -> not selectable.

## 57285274843545 Why didn't Mimikyu ex's Disguise Ability prevent damage from an attack?
Does NOT work vs "Attacks that ignore effects (such as Ledian's Swift attack)" or when "Abilities that nullify Disguise are in effect (such as Alolan Muk's Power of Alchemy Ability)".
Works "The first time it takes damage from an attack that does not nullify or ignore effects" and "On the first attack after an Ability-nullifying effect has worn off."

## 55980981811097 Why didn't Mimikyu ex's Disguise Ability prevent damage?
"Mimikyu ex's Disguise Ability does not prevent damage that doesn't come from attacks."
Not blocked: "Damage from Special Conditions (such as Poisoned or Burned)"; "Damage from Pokémon Abilities (such as Greninja's Water Shuriken)"; "Damage from Pokémon Tools (such as Rocky Helmet)".

## 57281623683481 Why didn't my opponent's Pokémon take damage from Mismagius's Cursed Prose after it retreated to the Bench?
Cursed Prose "applies an effect to the target Pokémon that causes it to take 90 damage at the end of your opponent's next turn."
"all effects applied to a Pokémon are removed when that Pokémon retreats to the Bench."
"if the Defending Pokémon hit by Cursed Prose retreats before the end of your opponent's next turn, the attack's effect will be removed and the Pokémon will not take damage."

## JA original of 57286049312665 (damage calculation) — verbatim
ダメージ計算は、以下の順番で行われます。
1.ワザの基本ダメージを計算
2.ワザを使うポケモンにかかっている効果を計算
3.弱点のダメージを計算
4.ワザを受けるポケモンにかかっている効果を計算
例）スタジアム「結界のフィールド」が場にある状態で、「受けるワザのダメージを-20する」効果をもつ相手のポケモンに、50ダメージのワザで弱点を計算するとき、相手のバトルポケモンが受けるダメージは「80」です。 50×2-20=80

## 57286218211225 (JA only) Why did the opponent's Ability work when Budew used Itchy Pollen (ひりひりパウダー)?
「ワザを使った後にはたらく効果」は、「ひりひりパウダー」の効果を受けます
  例）クリムガン(Druddigon)の特性さめはだ(Rough Skin: when damaged by an attack in the Active Spot, 20 damage to the attacker) vs ひりひりパウダー → Budew takes NO damage.
「ワザを使う前からはたらいている効果」は、「ひりひりパウダー」の効果を受けません
  例）グレンアルマex(Armarouge ex) 特性そうこう(-30 from attacks) vs ひりひりパウダー → the -30 still applies to that attack;
  but if Armarouge ex stays Active and is attacked again, it takes the full damage (its Ability is gone after the first Itchy Pollen).
=> ordering evidence: pre-existing passive modifiers apply to the damage; the attack's own effect then applies; then "after the attack" triggered effects (Rough Skin) — which the attack's effect can already suppress.

## 57281556361113 (JA duplicate of Cursed Prose)

## 57284110688921 Jolteon ex Electromagnetic Wall
"only takes effect when Energy is attached to a Pokémon from the Energy Zone." Activates: normal Energy Zone attachment; card effect attaching from the Energy Zone. Does not: from discard pile; moved from one Pokémon to another.

## 57283495239193 Prank Spinner
"randomly chooses only one card from either your hand or your opponent's hand to send back to the deck."

## 57281428279193 Scizor Gale Thrust (+50 if moved Bench->Active this turn)
Applies when Scizor is moved from your Bench to Active during your turn. NOT when your Pokémon was KO'd on the opponent's turn and Scizor was promoted; NOT when Scyther moved up and then evolved into Scizor that turn.

## 53604957483289 Buzzwole ex Big Beat
"any effects on a Pokémon are removed when it moves to the Bench." (so the can't-use-next-turn restriction is cleared by benching and returning)

## 57282177269273 Points when discarded by an effect
"No, a player does not get points if an opposing Pokémon in play is discarded due to an effect." "Points are gotten only when an opponent's Pokémon is Knocked Out." (example: Guzzlord's attack)
