# Main-session findings (coordinator's own fetches), 2026-09-21

## pokemon-zone how-to-play (https://www.pokemon-zone.com/articles/how-to-play-pokemon-tcg-pocket/)
- "Pokémon Checkup is a step that happens after the end of each turn."
- "If both players' Pokémon are affected by Special Conditions, the player whose turn just ended checks their Special Conditions first."
- "Any Pokémon that has no HP remaining at the end of Pokémon Checkup is Knocked Out."
- Order within one Pokémon: Poisoned, Burned, Asleep, Paralyzed.
- Win: points; opponent can't promote after Active KO. "If both players have a Pokémon on their bench that they can promote, the game ends in a tie." "If one player has no benched Pokémon left to promote, their opponent fulfills two win conditions to their one, and thus, their opponent wins the game."
- Turn: 1 draw (mandatory); 2 "If the top spot of the Energy Zone is empty, the Energy from the inner circle is promoted to the top, while a new Energy from the Zone takes its place in the inner circle"; actions; attack; "The turn ends immediately after resolving the attack and its effects".
- Energy: "Once per turn, you can manually attach the larger Energy ... If you don't, it gets discarded at the end of your turn."
- Setup: Active placed face-down, both confirm, flip face-up.
- "You also don't draw any more card from your deck if your hand contains 10 cards or more."
- Timers: 90 s per turn; "The match starts with a 20 minute timer."
- First turn: "a player can attack on their first turn"; starting player gets no Energy.

- "if one player has at least a single Pokemon the Bench and the other doesn't, then they're declared the winner, regardless of who inflicted the final blow."
- "A tie also happens even if one player scores four Points ... so long as both Actives are knocked out at the same time and the Benches are clear."

## Bulbapedia Battle (TCG Pocket) (https://bulbapedia.bulbagarden.net/wiki/Battle_(TCG_Pocket))
- "Each player is given a timer of 20 minutes total for all of their actions." "Each player is given 90 seconds for their turn."
- "If the player's timer runs out, the points of each player are checked." "If both players have the same number of points, the Battle ends in a tie."
- Ranked: "If the player's 90-second turn timer runs out, it gets halved the next turn."
- "If the player doesn't take any action, their turn is forced to end, the generated Energy is discarded if it wasn't attached ... and no Attack is used."
