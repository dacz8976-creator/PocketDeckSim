## Which record to rely on

For this game, X is the one a player could rely on more, though only narrowly: it has no major errors, while G has one. G's single major error is a wrong Energy target (Furfrou instead of Oricorio) on the opponent's turn 8, repeated in three board-state lines, which would mislead anyone tracking where the opponent's Energy sits. X's two errors are only wrong times (about 20 s and 10 s) for two manual Water attachments, with the right targets and counts. G is the richer record: it gives every hand, each Copycat draw, HP before and after each hit, Dustin's Card of the Game, and its times are the better ones on the turn 5 and turn 9 attachments. For attacks, damage, Knock Out, points, score and ending the two are identical and correct. Use G for detail and timing, but double-check its opponent Energy ledger against X. The cheap Gemini opponent sheet should not be used without checking, as it misnames Furfrou.
