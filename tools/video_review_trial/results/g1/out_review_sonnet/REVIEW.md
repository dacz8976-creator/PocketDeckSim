All times are frame-file seconds. "Approx." means the action sits in a short window of frames and I did not pin the exact second.

## Header
- Deck: the QR / "Share With 2D Pattern Code" screen is shown at 0–1 s. The Battle screen at 2 s shows the deck "New deck 17" (Master Ball Rank, 970 pts, Season 84b SP). The deck name is not shown beyond "New deck 17".
- Who went first: **Ryze (opponent)**. "Going second" appears at 25 s for Dustin. The result screen says "Went second".
- Final score: **Dustin 3 – Ryze 2**. Dustin won ("Victory!", about 349–357 s).
- End: points. Dustin reached 3 points on Turn 12 by Knocking Out Hitmonchan ex (+2).
- Global turns: **12** (result screen "Turns played 12").
